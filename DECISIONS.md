# Design decisions & assumptions

Living record of every assumption behind `src/params.ts`. Params cite these IDs through their `ref` field.
**Status:** ✅ decided · 🔶 provisional (Phase 2 will confirm) · ❓ needs Eric's input.
Units are inches, pounds and degrees throughout.

## Conventions

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| C-1 | **Model frame:** +X forward (toward the bridle), +Y left, +Z up. The origin is on the ground under the rear-axle center. The viewer uses the same Z-up frame, so no frame conversion happens anywhere. | ✅ |
| C-2 | **Single source of truth:** `src/params.ts` → `src/model/*` → `Part[]`. Model and analysis code is plain TS with no three.js imports, so it runs headless in tests. | ✅ |
| C-3 | Enumerated choices are stored as numbers so every param serializes into the shareable URL hash. | ✅ |
| C-4 | Each param has a `status`: locked, verify, assumed or explore. `validateParams()` rejects impossible combinations, such as a nose wider than the cabin, a goal rider lighter than the operating rider, or a deck below the axle stack. The panel shows those problems live. | ✅ |
| C-5 | **One Part = one physical piece.** `massLb` is per piece. Identical pieces share a `partNo`, and the cut list and BOM aggregate on it. Every part names its rigid `body` (chassis / steer / wheel-xx / rider / cargo / truck) for articulation and the sim. | ✅ |
| C-6 | **The displayed steer angle is view state, not a design param.** It lives in the viewer toolbar, is clamped to ± lock, and is never stored in the URL. | ✅ |

## Loads & factors

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | **Two rider masses.** The *operating* rider is **150 lb**, the largest realistic scout, and every check must pass at it. The *goal* rider is **200 lb** (adult or stretch case). Structure is sized to the goal rider. Stability and brake are reported at both. If 200 lb would force a compromise, it is flagged rather than designed around. (Eric, 2026-10-10.) | ✅ |
| D-2 | **Structural load case:** (goal rider 200 + cargo 25) × 3 = **675 lbf** payload. Members that also carry the cart's own weight (axles, axle beams, kingpin, turntable) add **cart mass × 3**: **+265 lbf** for the lean ~88 lb (13") design, **+365 lbf** for the ~121 lb default (W-1). Wood gets SF 2 on top. Steel is checked against yield / 2. | ✅ |
| D-3 | **Seated rider model:** CG **9.5" above the seat** and **4" ahead of the H-point** (legs extended forward). H-point to foot stop is **36"**, about 0.53 × a 68" stature with knees slightly bent. These are anthropometric approximations, not measurements. | 🔶 |
| D-4 | **White oak**, USDA Wood Handbook FPL-GTR-282, Table 5-3a, clear wood at 12% MC: SG 0.68 → **47 lb/ft³**; MOR **15,200 psi**; MOE **1.78 Mpsi**; shear ∥ **2,000 psi**; compression ∥ **7,440 psi**; compression ⊥ **1,070 psi**. Wet-service factors (NDS C_M) are **0.85** on strength and **0.90** on stiffness. **Design bending stress** = 15,200 × 0.85 / 2 = **6,460 psi** at dynamic load. Compression ⊥ governs axle grooves, kingpin bearing and bolt crush. We select clear, straight-grained stock, so no grade knock-down. **Steel:** 1018 CD, yield taken as 50 ksi (book value 54), E = 29 Mpsi. **Kingpin** is SAE grade 8 at 130 ksi. 18-8 stainless at 30–65 ksi is the alternative and would need a larger diameter. | 🔶 |
| D-5 | **Plywood:** okoume marine (BS 1088) at **33 lb/ft³**, effective E 0.9 Mpsi and MOR 5,000 psi with face grain along the span. Exterior Baltic birch is the alternative: **42 lb/ft³**, 1.3 Mpsi, 7,000 psi. These are typical published panel values, and Phase 2 uses them only for deck-span checks. Okoume is **~21% lighter** per panel. | 🔶 |
| D-14 | **Pull loads.** Steady pull is rolling resistance (Crr ≈ 0.06 on dirt × ~290 lb ≈ 17 lbf) plus grade (10% grade ≈ +29 lbf), so **~20–50 lbf**. Design peak is **150 lbf**, for 4–6 scouts surging. It is applied at a puller hand height of 30". The short oak **tongue** (24" ahead of the kingpin) is the default because it gives a steering lever arm and keeps the bridle away from the front tires. Phase 2 compares it with a bridle tied to the axle ends. | 🔶 |

## Truck fit & transport

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D-6 | **Track width is set by the truck.** With the wheels *beside* the wheel wells, the wheel assembly's outer half-width sets the limit. That half-width is max(tire/2 = 2", hub/2 + axle end = 1.5 + 1 = 2.5") = **2.5"**. So track + 2 × 2.5 + 2 × 0.5 clearance ≤ 41.5, giving **max track 35.5"** (the default). `validateParams` warns past it. **"Straddle" alternative:** put the front wheels *ahead of* the wells and the rear wheels *behind* them, and the track is limited only by the 57" floor. That allows roughly a 44–48" track, a large stability gain. With the default well position (18–52" from the bulkhead), front wheel center ≤ 18 − 6.5 − 0.5 = 11" and rear wheel center ≥ 52 + 7 = 59". That needs **wheelbase ≥ 48" with zero fore-aft slack**, and puts the rear tires across the tailgate hinge gap. The body must also stay under 41.5" wide below the 10" well height. **This depends on measured well positions (Q-1).** | ❓ |
| D-7 | **Loading is a two-person lift**, with no ramps and no winch. With the heavy design (D-21), transport weight with the removables off is **~110 lb, about 55 lb per person**. That's fine for two adults, but too much for scouts (Q-7). Fit lift handles at both ends in Phase 3. A 32" load height at ≤ 20° needs a ramp ≥ 94" long, longer than the cart. The axle beams and grab handles are the lift points. | ✅ |
| D-8 | **Stowage length** = bed 60.5 + tailgate 20 = **80.5"**. The default **body** is 66" long. With the tongue fitted the cart is 82", so the **tongue must pin off or fold up** for transport (D-9). | 🔶 |
| D-9 | **Removable for transport:** rear push handle, seat back, tongue, prow ornament and signs. They attach with knobs, pins or threaded inserts. The truck-fit check runs both with and without them. | ✅ |

## Structure & layout

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D-10 | **Dead (non-rotating) axles.** Hand-truck wheels have sealed bearings in the hub, so the axle can be clamped to the oak beam. That avoids pillow blocks, which are cast iron at ~1.5 lb each. **Options:** a through rod, or **stub axles**, ~8" of rod per wheel clamped in a ≥ 2½"-deep beam. Stubs save ~6 lb of steel but need a heavier beam, and on the lean budget (W-1) a ⅝" through rod on lean beams comes out ~2 lb lighter overall. Phase 2 decides with the stress check. A live axle is needed only for the wood-drum brake. | 🔶 |
| D-11 | **Wheels: 13" (4.10/3.50-6) by default.** Wheel diameter is a continuous param from 10 to 13". The common stock sizes are 10" (4.10/3.50-4), ~12" (3.00-4) and 13", with ¾" or ⅝" ball-bearing bores. Mass is **~5.5 lb per 13" wheel and ~4 lb per 10" wheel**. Flat-free foam tires are an option at about +1.25 lb per wheel. All values are placeholders until a catalog part is chosen (Q-4). | 🔶 ❓ |
| D-12 | **Steering:** wagon steer on a 12" square, 1000 lb lazy-susan turntable (~⅜" stack), with a ½" grade-8 kingpin. **The turntable is not moment-rated**, so the roll moment from one front wheel hitting a bump must go through oak rub blocks or side-bearers at the beam ends, not through the bearing. Mechanical stops are oak blocks on the beam bearing on the frame, with lock at 40° (explored). | 🔶 |
| D-13 | **Brake:** shoes on the **rear tire treads** (default), with a wood drum as the alternative. Shoe friction acts at the tire radius, so it is 1:1 with ground force: no drum and no live axle. One lever works both shoes through a cross shaft, with a ratchet detent for parking. Linkage efficiency is taken as **0.8**. The **10:1** default covers the worst case in the hand-calc table (9.3:1 needed). | 🔶 |
| D-15 | **Vertical stack at the axles.** By default the axle beam is **underslung**: the axle sits in a groove on top of the beam, held by U-bolts. With a 13" wheel the beam spans z = 4.4–6.9"; that is ≥ 3.5" clearance ✅. **Front:** beam 6.9 + turntable 0.38 + crossmember 1.75 + ply 0.35 puts the deck at **≥ 9.35"** over the front axle. The main deck is 9", so the forward deck steps up **0.5"** (`frontDeckRaiseIn`). **Rear:** beam + ply needs only 7.2", so the rear beam can be recessed between the rails. **Front-wheel swing:** at 40° lock the inner tire's rear-inner corner reaches **(32.9, ±7.9)**, about 15" behind the front axle (35.5" track). See A-4 for the swing clearance. The hull narrows to **14"**, and the full-width cabin ends **18.5" behind the front axle**. The narrow section is the rider's foot well. **Rails** (¾ × 3, ladder option) span z ≈ 5.6–8.6" under a 9" deck. They notch over the rear axle beam, whose top is at 6.9". **10" wheels:** r = 5, so an underslung 2"-deep beam bottoms out at 3.3", under the 3.5" minimum. A 10" build needs a 1¾" beam or an overslung beam. | 🔶 |
| D-16 | **Ground and friction:** minimum ground clearance is **3.5"** (roots, trail crown). Tire-to-ground μ is **0.5** (damp packed dirt or gravel); wet leaves can be ~0.3. Shoe-to-tire μ is **0.4** (wet). | 🔶 |
| D-17 | **Primary structure, two candidates.** (a) A **ladder frame**: two ¾ × 3" oak rails plus crossmembers, with the deck on top. (b) **Hull-as-beam**: the sides are deep girders (oak chine + oak gunwale + captured ply web), and floor joists carry the deck between them. That deletes the rails, saving ~8 lb, and is how the "boat" character pays for itself. The max unsupported deck span (joist spacing) is 12" by default. Phase 2 sizes both against D-2 and L/300. | 🔶 |
| D-18 | **Seat:** a 2" closed-cell foam cushion (vinyl-covered, drains), with a back 16" tall reclined 15°, removable for transport. The H-point sits 12" ahead of the rear axle (explored). | 🔶 |
| D-20 | **Finish mass is computed from surface area**, not as a percentage. Two epoxy saturation coats come to ~0.03 lb/ft² each (WEST System: ~1 gal ≈ 9 lb mixed covers ~300 ft² per coat on sanded hardwood). Three spar-varnish coats add ~0.01 lb/ft² each. Total ≈ **0.09 lb/ft²**, times the total wood surface (both faces, edges included), which is ~60–70 ft² here, or **~6 lb**. | 🔶 |
| D-19 | **Storage:** about **1,800 in³** (~1 ft³) of interior volume for the patrol's shared kit: first-aid kit ~150, 4 L of water ~300, 6 rain shells ~400, lights ~60, food ~200, spare layers ~600, map, compass and fire kit ~60. Default location is **behind the seat, over the rear axle**. Space there is about 24" wide × 12" fore-aft, so the box is ~6½" tall, and it doubles as the seat-back base. Moving the 25 lb from mid-cart to over the axle adds only ~8 lb of rear-wheel load. It's a small help to the rear brake, not a fix. Over the front wheels the box would have to sit above the 13" tire. | 🔶 |

## Decisions from the Phase 0 checkpoint (Eric, 2026-10-10)

| D-25 | **Kingpin leads the front axle by 1.25"** (critic Phase 1 r1, H1). A kingpin on the axle centerline would have to pass through the ¾" axle rod. Moving it ahead clears the rod (≥ ⅞" between centers for ½" + ¾" plus web), and gives the trailing axle caster-like **self-centring under pull**, which the bridle steering wants anyway. The wheelbase is still measured axle to axle. The turntable centers on the kingpin. | ✅ |
| D-26 | **Front geometry after the Phase 1 critic rounds** (supersedes the numbers in D-12, D-15, D-25 and A-4; those entries keep their reasoning):
- **Kingpin lead** is **1.0"**.
- **Front axle beam** is **3.5"** fore-aft. It is centerd on the rod + kingpin pair, which leaves ≥ ¾" of wood at each end. The rear beam stays 2½".
- **Steering stops** are square posts on the turntable plate. They land flat on the aft bolster rail at exactly ± lock.
- **Side-bearers** are 1½" oak blocks mortised into the beam, with a UHMW face and 1/16" running clearance.

**Tension to resolve in Phase 2:** lock, nose width (feet), turning radius and track all trade against each other. A wider track (straddle stowage) also pushes the shoulder aft. | 🔶 |

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D-31 | **Steering stops are redesigned** (supersedes the D-12 / D-26 oak posts; critic Phase 2 r2 A3, ~2,150 lbf on 1.5 in², ≈ 1,440 psi against 358).<br>- **Dogs.** Two oak blocks (1½" thick, 2.74" long, 1.72" tall) stand on the beam inside the bolster opening, one on each side of the crossbar, with a ⅛" A36 steel face plate on each side. Each dog stops both locks. Two ⅜" through-bolts hold each down through the lazy-susan plate into the beam; fender washers (1½" OD) under the beam.<br>- **Stop blocks.** Four oak wedges (chassis) in the corners of the opening, glued and screwed to the fore/aft bolster rail and the crossbar. Each block face is the plane the dog's steel face reaches at exactly ±lock, so contact is flat, face on face, at lock and the gap before lock closes with no earlier touch (`src/model/stops.ts`; the tests pin first contact at ±lock and a strictly clear sweep).<br>- **A couple.** The right dog and blocks are the left ones turned 180° about the kingpin, so two stops close at either lock and the yaw torque is not a net force on the kingpin. Design basis: the stiffer stop takes **60%** (param `steerStopLoadShare`), the other 40%, to allow for fit-up tolerance. The stops must be shimmed so both close. If one took everything (share 1) the net force adds 1.5 kip to the kingpin and the check fails.<br>- **Why faces are radial.** The lever arm of a stop force is the face coordinate s, not the offset of the face from the kingpin, so a long radial face (s = 2.91…5.65") gets r_eff = 4.43" against the 3.0" of the old flat-on-rail post. Rigid rotation makes the pressure ∝ s (triangular): p_max = 3·T₁·s₂/(h(s₂³−s₁³)).<br>- **Result (goal rider).** T = 6.7k lb·in (one-wheel 3" curb strike: H_w = 377 lbf at y_w = 17.75"), T₁ = 4.0k lb·in, F = 907 lbf per stop, peak face bearing 255 psi (limit 358), wood behind the face ≥ ½" everywhere, dog bolts 4.1 ksi shear / interaction 0.49, washer 275 psi. The kingpin sees ≤ 679 lbf against the 752 lbf the two-wheel strike already puts on it (90%, so the row is marginal). Check `steer-stops`.<br>- **Limits.** The wedge is drawn as its bounding rectangle in the overlap tests. The dogs bolt through the lazy-susan plate: use a plate with an open-center or access holes (ask the vendor) and confirm hole pull-through. Toe crush of the dog on the plate is not modeled. | 🔶 |
| D-32 | **Kingpin: peak bearing, pitch path and ligaments** (critic Phase 2 r2 A1, A2).<br>- **Peak bearing (`kingpin-peak`).** A rigid pin in two oak members (beam 2.50" deep, crossbar 1.78", 0.375" gap) with the horizontal load H crossing between them has a **linear** pressure that reverses sign in each member. Peak line load q = (H/D)(1 + 6·a_c·D²/Σ), a_c = g + (D₁+D₂)/2, Σ = D₁³ + D₂³. At the defaults that is **5.4× the average in the beam and 3.2× in the crossbar** (goal: 1,309 and 1,098 psi on the 1¼" sleeve OD). The pin on the sleeve bore is 2,181 psi (limit 16.2 ksi). Pin bending from integrating the line loads is 15.6 ksi (limit 65). The old average rows stay in `kingpin-strike`.<br>- **Allowable (owner to accept).** The peak is a local pin-embedment pressure, so it is held to the oak **dowel-bearing** allowable 1,700 psi (4,000 × 0.85 / 2), not the 358 psi area-bearing value that governs the average rows. Result: 0.77 utilization at the goal rider. If the owner holds peaks to 358 psi too, the beam peak is 3.65× and the crossbar 3.06× over; the fix would be a deeper crossbar or a larger gap, or a bigger sleeve OD. The 3× dynamic factor is still stacked on tanφ: an envelope, not an impact analysis.<br>- **Pitch path (`pitch-path`).** The turntable is not moment-rated, so the path is defined and checked. The strike's pitch moment M_p = H × |axle height − beam mid-depth| is returned as a vertical couple between the **fore and aft bolster rails** (centers 10.5" apart) through the plate margins (3.5 psi): the bearing ring and the pin carry no moment. The trail moment (the load acts at the pin, 1.5" ahead of the rod) twists the beam about the rod and is held by the **front U-bolt legs** and the plate under the beam (leg tension 0.9 ksi, plate 26 psi). Fore-aft side bearers on the beam are not possible: the beam is only 4" fore-aft, the rod groove takes 0.75" of it, and the plate margins overhang it. Open item: the stiffness of the lazy-susan plate margins that overhang the beam (vendor).<br>- **Ligaments (`kingpin-split`).** Block-shear (plug) shear-out τ = F/(2·e·D) of the beam front ligament (0.75", strike), the web between bore and rod groove (0.5", rope pull) and the crossbar side ligaments (0.875", lateral load μ·V) and end ligament. All ≤ 24% of 850 psi. This is not NDS cleavage; the crossbar edge distance (e/d_bore = 0.7) is far below the 4·d rule, so the side-ligament row is the one to watch. | 🔶 |
| D-33 | **Bridle ties and bolster fasteners** (critic Phase 2 r2 A4, M6, M7).<br>- **Tie points.** A ⅜" forged shoulder eye bolt through the **front face** of the front beam on each side (axis fore-aft, along the rope), nut and washer on the aft face, **1" inboard of the U-bolt plate** (13.25" off center). The rope load goes into the beam and not into the axle rod (closes critic A4). Rope legs start at the eye centers.<br>- **Tie load (`bridle-tie`).** 150 lbf on ONE leg along the taut rope to the pullers' hands at the **upper bound 40"** (param `bridleTieHandHeightMaxIn`), 96" beyond the ring: 16.4° above horizontal. Axial 143 lbf, transverse 45 lbf bending the shank over the 1" eye offset: 10.5 ksi against 18 ksi (yield/2), washer 225 psi (limit 358). Confirm the bought eye bolt's working-load limit ≥ 150 lbf at that angle.<br>- **Rope spec** goes on the BOM line: ½" braided polyester, tails ≥ 8 ft, tied low.<br>- **Bolster fasteners.** The ring is tied to the hull by **4 × ⅜" carriage bolts** (was an assumed 4 × ¼") through the nose deck, the fore/aft rails and the turntable top plate. They are structured `bolt` joints on `bolster-fore` / `bolster-aft` (with positions) that `roll-bolster-attach` reads. A new row adds the stops' yaw-torque couple, F = T·r_max/Σr² + H/n: 3.5 ksi against 10.4 ksi. | 🔶 |
| D-34 | **Front mounting details** (critic Phase 2 r2 / recommendation items M8, M10, M11).<br>- **Front U-bolts.** A ⅜" square U-bolt with its bridge over the axle rod on top of the underslung beam, legs down **around** the beam faces (nothing drilled through the beam), a ⅜" steel plate under the beam, nuts below the plate (`clamp-F?1` = U-bolt, `clamp-F?2` = plate). The nuts are now the lowest point at 3.6" (limit 3.5"): `uboltNutAllowanceIn` and a validator warning keep that honest. +1.3 lb. The **rear** clamps are unchanged (same orientation problem, workstream C territory).<br>- **Access panel.** A 4½" × 2¾" panel of the nose-deck ply, flush with the deck over the kingpin nut, four #10-24 stainless machine screws into inserts epoxied in the crossbar top (the nut sits in a counterbore). Edges sealed. It stays on in transport (it is removable for service, so not flagged `removable`). `Layout.accessPanel` gives B the opening to cut.<br>- **Specs (M11).** Sleeve: 1¼" OD × ¼" wall DOM tube (ID 0.750", hone to +0.003" if needed), pressed and epoxied into reamed bores. Wheels: hand-truck, ¾" bore, 3" hub, sealed ball bearings (or flanged bronze bushings), collar + cotter pin. Kingpin: SAE grade 8 hex bolt with hardened washers and a lock nut. All recorded in `sourcing` / `notes`. | 🔶 |
| D-35 | **Captured-ply hull, real joinery.** The okoume web enters a **3/8" × (ply + 1/64") groove** in the chine (top face) and the gunwale (underside), leaving ≥ 0.1" of oak each side (validator warns below that). The decks run **1/4" into a rabbet in each chine and a dado in each transom**; the web ends enter **stopped 1/4" dados** in the transoms. The transoms are now **full width** (outside width, 1/2" thick, wrapping the side ends), so the sides run between the transom faces. XL (DF 700) is selected automatically for thick stock (≥ 2.5") but nothing at the current 1/2"–1-3/4" sizes needs it. Joints are stored on **both** mated parts with `at` in each part's local frame; `joinerySummary()` counts each once. The flared (D-6) groove follows the tilt of the web. | 🔶 |
| D-36 | **Bent-lam prow.** A `sweep` of **6 × 1/8" white-oak strips (3/4" thick) × 1-1/2" wide**: a 2.5" straight leg against the front face of the front transom (above the lift slot), then a Bezier to **18" above the gunwale line, 7-3/4" ahead of the transom**. ## Analysis-criterion interpretations

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A-4 | **Front-wheel swing clearance.** At lock δ, with a = front track / 2, w = tire width and r = tire radius, all relative to the kingpin: the inner tire's rear-inner corner sits at **y = (a − w/2)·cos δ − r·sin δ**. The rear-most tire extent behind the kingpin is **(a + w/2)·sin δ + r·cos δ**. Both are worst at full lock, since δ stays below atan((a + w/2)/r) ≈ 72°. Requirements: nose half-width ≤ y − c, and the full-width cabin ends ≥ (rear extent + c) behind the front axle, with c = steering clearance 0.75". Defaults (35.5" track, 40°): y = 7.89, so nose ≤ 14.3" (14 ✅). Rear extent = 17.67, so the shoulder must be ≥ 18.42" back (18.5 ✅). The tire is treated as a rectangle at full radius, which is conservative. `validateParams` enforces both. | ✅ |
| A-1 | ✅ **Accepted by Eric 2026-10-10: pass/fail = static stability factor (T/2)/h ≥ 1.5 × μ_ground; v_rollover against radius is reported for assessment in the final version.** Background: **the brief's turn-rollover target can't be reached.** The brief asks for v_rollover ≥ 3 × 6 mph at minimum radius. Rollover lateral acceleration is (T/2)/h ≈ **1.05 g** here, and 18 mph at a ~5 ft radius needs **~4.4 g**. **Proposed replacement:** (i) v_rollover ≥ **1.5 × jog speed** at minimum radius, evaluated on the **outer** support edge; (ii) **slide before tip**: μ_ground < (T/2)/h, so on flat ground the cart skids rather than rolls; (iii) a plot of v_rollover against radius. **Current hand calc: 1.44–1.52×** depending on mass (table below), so it **straddles the 1.5× line**. But at μ 0.5 the cart *slides* at ~6 mph in that 5 ft turn, well before it could roll at ~9 mph. Untripped rollover on flat ground can't happen while μ < (T/2)/h. **Alternative criterion** (critic round 2): **static stability factor (T/2)/h ≥ 1.5 × μ_ground**, i.e. slides with 50% margin before tipping. Current value is 1.00–1.11 against 0.75 ✅. Recommend making this the pass/fail check and reporting v_roll as information. Fixes if Eric prefers the speed criterion: wider track (straddle, D-6), lower CG, or a 35° lock. A trip on a curb or root can't be checked statically, so the sim covers it qualitatively. | ❓ |
