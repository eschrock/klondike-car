## Engineering

**MUST-1 handle sockets: done.** `validateParams` errors when the riding pin hole is under 4 pin diameters (1.25 in) above the post foot and warns under 3 in of insertion (`handleInsertion(p)` in `params.ts`, held equal to `handleGeometry` by a test). A 12 in socket now errors (0.19 in), a 13.2 in one warns. The 14 in default gives 3.69 in of post and a 2.19 in (7 diameter) pin end distance. `handleSockets` caps the bearing strip at the real insertion and has a pin end-distance row. The `handleSocketDepthIn` note now says 14 in, 3.7 in of post and +0.46 lb (the old "1.7 in" text and the "+0.2 lb" are gone). A 12 in rear side would also recreate the defect (a lower transom sinks the parked handle 11.8 in), and the new check catches that too (the sheer variant test now uses an 18 in socket).

**MUST-2 bridle: done.** No shackle. The 3/8 in nylon three-strand rope runs through the bronze-sleeved hole once, round the tongue tip, and is tied with a bowline round its own standing part (a single strand passes the 5/8 in bore: 5/8 x 1 in oil-bronze sleeve bearing, verify the stock size). The ROPE is the documented weak link: rated 3,700 lbf new (verify), 2,220 lbf at the bowline (60%), working load limit 370 lbf (10%) against the 150 lbf design pull (row 0.41). `bridle-eye` now has a SNATCH case: F = v sqrt(k m), 4 mph, 25 lb/in for nylon with the pullers (verify by a luggage-scale stretch test), about 310 to 330 lbf for the 150 to 200 lb rider (a stiff polyester rope at 100 lb/in would be 620 to 670); against the ultimate strengths with no safety factors (stem, shear-out, net section, bolts, knot) everything is under 0.15; the sleeve bearing (0.15x of its allowable) and the nut washers (0.65x) dent and that is stated as acceptable. The order-of-failure text is computed, not asserted: the first oak or steel detail to reach its ultimate would be the carriage bolts (yield) at about 2,226 lbf, essentially level with the rope's 2,220, so the rope goes first "only just" (the wording switches if a stronger rope is chosen). Optional 3 ft shock-cord section in the notes (set `bridleSnatchStiffLbIn` to about 10). Params replaced: `eyeBoltDiameterIn` and `eyeBoltYieldPsi` are gone; new `bridleRopeDiaIn`, `bridleRopeBreakLbf`, `bridleKnotEfficiency`, `bridleRopeWllFraction`, `bridleSnatchSpeedMph`, `bridleSnatchStiffLbIn`, `tongueSleeveBoreIn`. Side effect worth knowing: the overall length without the shackle is 1.07 in shorter (68.8 in), the beside window now reaches 55.25 in wheelbase and the assembled sled straddles the wheel wells at 52 in.

**S-2 front-beam eye bolt: done.** `steerGeometry.beamZ` is the middle of the ligament under the 0.70 in stub groove (3.85 in at the defaults, was 4.2 in mid depth); `front-beam` has a bending row for the net section at the eye bolt (0.42 normal, 0.30 hard).

**N-1 side web row: done.** New `side-web` row (hull.ts, Structure / strength light): the free 4 mm panel between the bands as a strip fixed at the bands, at its tallest unsupported section (8.7 in at x = -0.7; the transom holds the rest; kick 1,279 psi, cargo 61 psi against the 2,125 psi ply design stress), under a 50 lbf kick on a 6 in patch (`sideKickLbf`, `sideKickPatchIn`) and under cargo pressure (`cargoSideG` 0.5 g over `cargoSideContactIn` 36 in): 0.60. A person sitting on the gunwale is not covered, and the row says so. It is a stress row; there is no deflection limit (the web will drum).

**S-5 cargo height rule: done.** New info row `cargo-height` (solves for the cargo center height: tip angle reaches the 40 degree limit at 13.8 in, slide-before-tip is lost at 17.0 in; 42.0 / 36.0 / 31.4 degrees at 12 / 18 / 24 in, matching the review), and the Simple view's cargo card says: "Load-height rule: heavy things low, on the floor, strapped down. Keep the middle of the load under 12 in above the floor (it tips at about 14 in); above about 17 in a fast turn can tip it instead of skidding." A placard on the sled itself is a Phase 4 item.

**S-4 / pre-order gate: done in notes + a validator warning.** The tire-type and `bumpWheelRateLbIn` notes say flat-free changes the bump rate, not just the mass, and that the squash test (0.55 in or more at 300 lb, at most about 560 lb/in) is a pre-order gate; `validateParams` warns for flat-free until the rate is set from a measurement. **DOM, not ERW:** the axle-tube sourcing text and the yield note say A513 Type 5, "NOT ordinary ERW" (about 45 ksi, takes axle-stress from 0.59 to 0.94), keep the mill cert, and (S-6) snug plus a quarter turn on the clamp bolts, re-check each event.

Not done from the engineering review (not asked): S-3 as written (the outer front plate is 1/4 in wider instead of 1/2 in, which is the same fix and takes the groove wall from 1.00 back to 0.88); N-2 (printed shim), N-3 (kingpin rake), N-4 (kingpin head counterbore), N-5 (parking text), N-6 (tire to body pad).

## Build

**M1 sandwich sides (D-61): done.** Each side is a full-height 4 mm web with an oak band glued on EACH face at the chine and at the gunwale; no grooves anywhere. Parts per side: `side-X-chine` and `side-X-gunwale` (the OUTER bands, their outer face is the hull face and keeps the old ids), `side-X-chine-in` and `side-X-gunwale-in` (inner bands), `side-X-web` (the web). Band thickness is derived, (side - panel ply) / 2 = 0.234 in, so 2 x 0.234 + 0.157 = the same 0.625 in. The web stops `sideWebCapIn` (1/8 in) under the gunwale top and the gap is filled with thickened epoxy so no ply edge shows. The inner chine band is 0.512 in short of the bottom (deck + clearance), so the deck rabbet comes from the strip being short: nothing is routed, the deck edge rises 0.234 in against the web (`Layout.deckEdge`). Gunwale bands are strips edge-bent to the sheer (radius about 170 in, under 0.5% strain) and plan-bent with the web, so the viewer's grain-follows-the-sheer is now true; the note gives the real slope at the transom (18.8 degrees, not the 9.7 mid-length figure). Cut list: bands are strips (`stock` = one strip, kind `bentlam`), the rails are `plies: 2` strips of 3/8 in (new `Part.plies`), with resaw and sanding-sled notes and 25% spares. Joints: band-to-web glue records, the web ends in the stopped dados, the four band ends butt-glued with a fillet at each end (a 0.234 in band takes no Domino), the chine-to-rail Dominos kept (the mortise runs across outer band and web edge). Analysis: `foot-stop` counts the web dado glue (4.3 in2) with the two rail tenons; `validateParams` has a band-thickness and inner-chine-height check replacing the groove-wall warning; the bend-radius check now uses one band. Params: `grooveDepthIn` removed (old links still load), `sideWebCapIn` added, `grooveSlopIn` kept for dados and the box. `HULL_STEP` and the part notes rewritten (S1, S10): dry-fit, measure transom dado to bulkhead dado, cut bands, webs and rails to that less 1/32 in, close the transom end first, then the bulkhead with a second batch.

**M2 axle clamps: done.** One bridge plate per station, 1/4 x 1.5 x 2-1/4 in 316 (6 plates, same 12 bolts), bolts at tube center +/- 0.75 in (`clampLegGapIn` 0.375; 0.17 in of oak to the groove wall, was 0.045), groove 0.70 in deep (`axleProudIn` 0.05, helper `axleGrooveDepthIn`) so the plate bears on the tube. Consequences handled: beam tops are 0.05 in lower (front stack, rear hanger seat, hold-down angle on the tube), roll pads and the rail clearance measured from the tube top, the groove depth feeds the beam and groove rows. The groove wall bearing (front-beam, hard hit, cargo) went to 1.00 with the shallower groove; a front OUTER plate 1.75 in wide (`clampPlateOuterWidthIn`, +0.08 lb) brings it to 0.88. Ligament around the bolts and the 0.70 in groove are in `d62-fixes.test.ts`.

**M3 brake hinge: done as documentation plus screws, the bevel is not drawn.** The knee top is BEVELED to the flap's applied slope (6 degrees, rising toward the front; 0.31 in higher at the front end) so the hinge is flat when applied and folds up 2.8 degrees released, never past flat; the model keeps the knee top flat at the pin height and the hinge as a flat box (stated in the notes). Screws are #10 x 3/4 (`brakeHingeScrewLenIn` 0.75, engagement 0.55 in), 8 per leaf (was 6): the shorter screw alone would have cut the green rider range from 230 to 210 lb, with 8 the row is 0.65. Notes say "hold a real hinge to it before cutting the first knee". Mount hardware and pad stay.

**M4 no screws into the web: done.** Two oak sign backing strips (0.5 x 1.5 in, `signBackerThickIn`) glued inside the web per side (`sign-backer-X1/X2`), each with two #10-32 x 3/8 threaded inserts for the knobs; they stay in the free web, under the inner gunwale band, and 0.27 in proud of the inner band faces. The flag blocks are epoxied to the inner bands (top block to the inner gunwale band, bottom to the inner chine band) and screwed only into the tube. A test asserts that no part has a screw, knob, insert or bolt joint to a web.

**M5 handle sockets: done.** 0.375 in access hole in the rear wall opposite each screw, relief groove 1/2 x 3/16 in down the transom face of each post (net section in the post-bending row; -0.07 lb), real inside dimensions in the text (0.75 x 1.25 in, slip 1/32 in; the 15/16 x 1-7/16 text is gone).

**S1, S2, S10: done.** S4 (seat-back pin): 2-1/4 in usable length, through-holes in the backer for the flat position, a spring-detent pin with a 1/16 in groove in a 1-1/2 in blind stop-block hole for the upright position (a ball-lock pin does not retain in a blind hole; verify the exact part and prove it in a scrap block). S5: UHMW at catalog sizes: 1/2 in thrust disc (was 9/16), 1/4 in roll pads (was 5/16) with `beamRailClearIn` 0.35 in (was a fixed 0.4) so the gap is 0.10 in (window 0.03 to 0.125), kingpin ordered as a stock length (1/2 in steps, cut with a cut-off wheel), the king-beam nut slot text agrees (at the top of the king beam). S7: the handlebow blank is a 5/4 x 6 board, 4.4 in wide in the stock (my geometry says the bow itself is 2.7 in from its lowest end to its highest edge, so 3 in would cut it, but the review's wider board lets the grain follow the arc to within 5 degrees instead of 11 to 14; I followed the review). S11: `fab: 'shaper-origin'` on the deck, webs, grab boards, bulkhead and hand-hold, transoms, box panels, sign blanks, brake flaps and the seat-back panel (no part is `cnc` any more). The stale texts (lid batten "take a foot", brake shoe "2 in", the bulkhead "CNC") were fixed; the "6 mm too thin for a spline" and "0.75 in above the lift slot" strings were no longer in the code.

## Owner decisions that arrived mid-round

**Sheet sources.** 4 mm and 6 mm okoume: Aircraft Spruce (Peachtree City, GA), will-call, full 4 x 8 sheets; the 12 mm deck: a local dealer. Params `thinSheetWidthIn` / `thinSheetLengthIn` (locked, 48 x 96, ref D-62), `sideWebScarf` (off), `plyScarfRatio` (8), `sideWebScarfXIn` (2.7). Each side web is ONE piece by default. Set the sheet to 23.5 x 47.5 (or `sideWebScarf` on) and each web becomes two halves (`side-X-web-1`, `-2`) with a recorded 8:1 scarf (1.26 in in 4 mm) at x = 2.7 in, hidden under the sign outside and next to a backing strip inside, bridged by all four oak bands, with the shop note on cutting it (block plane against a sanding jig, or the Shaper). `model/sheets.ts` + `sheets.test.ts`: every ply blank fits its sheet (checked for both sheet sizes; a scarf placed too far back fails the check), and a rough shelf-packing sheet count: **default: one 4 mm sheet and one 12 mm sheet (the deck, with the seat back cut from its offcut); no 6 mm sheet. With 2 x 4 sheets: three 4 mm sheets and one 12 mm.** The plywood params' notes carry the sources, the Baltic birch 12 mm deck fallback (about 3 to 4 lb heavier, epoxy-seal every face and edge), the solid-oak stocks (4/4 to 12/4, resaw and plane) and the finishing stack (stain with pre-stain conditioner or gel on okoume, epoxy seal, spar varnish).

**Frameless 12 mm seat back: done, no fallback needed.** The oak stiles, rails and 6 mm panel are gone (`seatBackPlyIn` 0.472, `seatBackThickIn` 0.472, status explore, ref D-60, D-62, note "Eric 2026-10-12: frameless 1/2 in from the deck offcut"). The panel is a 16 x 16 in rounded outline (top corners 2.5 in, bottom 1/2 in so the hinge and pin stations clear them), Shaper-cut, edge banded with 1/16 in oak veneer then epoxy-sealed. Hinges: 3 epoxied #10-32 x 3/8 threaded inserts per leaf in blind holes in the front face (never a ply edge); latch pins: 13/32 in clearance holes bored square at the two stations (3 in up the back, 0.625 in from the edge); the Dual Lock for the pad on the face. To support a bare panel a thin full-width oak crest strip (1.5 in tall, 0.18 lb) continues the stop-block wedges between the blocks (it stops short of the lid hinges, which stand 0.12 in proud of the same box end; the box end is also cut back 0.15 in more). The `seat-back` row was rewritten for the bare panel (panel as a strip above and below the crest, crest bearing, hinge load, insert pull-out per insert, latch-pin bearing and tear-out in ply, overhang deflection). Goal rider: 0.78, set by the pin tear-out (249 of 319 psi), then the panel bending 0.72 (1,537 of 2,125 psi); deflection 0.18 in; insert pull-out 0.10. WITHOUT the crest strip (two blocks 14.75 in apart) the panel would span them: my hand estimate is about 3,000 psi against 2,125 (not run in the code), so the strip is not optional. Mass +0.11 lb against the framed 6 mm back (2.83 against 2.71). Stow geometry: the flat back lies 0.472 in proud of the deck (was 0.75), still clears the grab boards and the foot brace; `seat-stow` is unchanged at 0.94. New params `seatInsertPulloutLbf` (150, verify by a bucket test on a scrap) and `seatPinPullLbf` (100).
