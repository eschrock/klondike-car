## Engineering

**MUST-2 bridle: done.** No shackle. The 3/8 in nylon three-strand rope runs through the bronze-sleeved hole once, round the tongue tip, and is tied with a bowline round its own standing part (a single strand passes the 5/8 in bore: 5/8 x 1 in oil-bronze sleeve bearing, verify the stock size). The order-of-failure text is computed, not asserted: the first oak or steel detail to reach its ultimate would be the carriage bolts (yield) at about 2,226 lbf, essentially level with the rope's 2,220, so the rope goes first "only just" (the wording switches if a stronger rope is chosen). ## The three MUSTs

**R2-M1 seat-back latch pins: done.**
- *Station.* `seatBackLatchHeightIn` default 3 -> **2** in up the panel (owner-approved). Flat x = 11.5 in, y = +/-7.375 in.
- *Pin.* The 2-1/4 in pin could not be used upright at 2 in: with its head on the panel face its tip would reach 1.78 in into a block that is 1.53 in thick along the pin (the old text put it 0.3 in into the box end). It is now a **1-1/2 in spring-detent pin in both positions** (both holes are blind, so the detent ball seats in a 1/16 in groove in each; a ball-lock pin needs a through hole). Upright: panel 0.47 in + **1.03 in into the block**. Flat: panel 0.47 + deck 0.47 + **0.56 in into the backer**. The pin model is now seated head-on-face with a 0.3 in head (the old model had its tip only 0.25 in behind the panel and 1.5 in of it standing off the front face).
- *Upright hole depth from the outline.* New `latchGeometry(p, L)` in `seat.ts`: the stop block is 1.53 in thick along the pin axis at 2 in up (rear face of the panel to the box end; at 3 in it was 1.27 in, at the review's 1.16 in figure the old 1.5 in hole ran through). Hole **1-1/16 in deep** (tip depth + 0.03 in, up to 1/16 in), groove at 0.73 in, **0.41 in of oak left behind it** (limit 1/4 in). The hole and groove numbers are in the stop-block pin joint, the pin notes and the `seat-back` row. If a steeper lean or a higher latch height would thin the block, `seatBackSupport` steps the station down a quarter inch at a time to a 3/4 in floor (a test sweeps lean 3-30 degrees and latch 1.5-6 in), and the `seat-back` row has two new details, "oak left behind the upright latch hole" and "oak left under the flat latch hole" (limit 1/4 in each), so a design that cannot take the pin FAILS instead of drilling into the box.
- *Flat position.* The one-piece 16.5 x 3 x 0.75 in backer is replaced by **two 1 in thick pads** (`seat-hinge-backer-L/R`, 3 in wide centered on each station, 4 pin diameters either side of the pin). `hingeBacker` runs from 0.5 in behind the hinge axis forward to the **face of the next joist (`joist-3`, x = 12.47 in), butted and glued**, so the flat pin (x = 11.5 in) has 2.5 in of pad behind it and a continuous joist behind the glue line. The blind hole is 5/8 in deep with the groove 0.26 in below the deck, 0.375 in of oak under it. The hinge screws (#8 x 1-1/4) bite 0.72 in of the 1 in pad (they used to stop 0.03 in short of coming through the 3/4 in strip). The pads weigh 0.5 lb less than the strip.
- *Flip geometry.* `applySeatStow` puts the flat pin seated head-up on the panel's rear face (it was 0.5 in above it); the seat-stow tests (cushion fit, the back clear of the foot brace and grab boards, seat-stow 0.94) are unchanged. The flat back still lies 0.472 in proud, from x = 9.5 to 25.5 in.
- *Seat-back row.* Pin bearing 565 of 2,125 psi (0.27) and tear-out 249 of 319 psi (0.78) are unchanged: both depend on the panel thickness and the 0.625 in from the hole to the panel edge, not on the station height. Goal rider 0.78, operating rider lower; no row above 0.9.
- *Pin head vs the cushion.* The pin station (2 in up the back, z = 10.05 in) is now at cushion-top height (10.0 in). The 0.3 in head stands inside the 3/8 in margin the cushion keeps off the back, with about 0.1 in to spare. **Verify with the real pin**: if the head touches the foam, notch the cushion's two rear corners.

**R2-M2 handle-socket access holes: done, 5/8 in.** Picked the simpler of the two options: one plain 5/8 in Forstner or step-bit hole through the 1/8 in rear wall opposite each screw (the 0.44 in button head and 0.5 in nylon washer pass with 1/16 in a side; 0.44 in of wall is left each side on the 1.5 in face).
