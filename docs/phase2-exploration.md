# Phase 2 parameter-space exploration

Generated by `scripts/explore.ts` (see Method). Numbers below are reproducible; the Observations section is hand-written and preserved on re-runs.

## Method

- Everything runs with **autoNoseGeometry on** (D-26): nose width and shoulder are derived per design from front track, lock and lead, so the Phase 1 coupling (C1) does not cap the sweep. All designs go through `validateParams`; designs with a validator **error** are skipped and counted ("invalid").
- Each design is evaluated with `runChecks` (resolved params, goal-rider model, both rider cases) plus `checkTruckFit` (all modes and configs, 0.25 in search step) computed once and reused.
- **Margins** are fractional: (value - limit)/limit for ">=" checks, (limit - value)/limit for "<=" checks, taken at the **worse of the 150 lb operating and 200 lb goal case**. Negative (bold) means a fail in at least one case; under +10% is "marginal". pitch-fwd has a limit of 0 deg, so its margin is degrees of slope headroom divided by the 15 deg design slope. v-roll is info only (A-1), shown for reference. beams = worse of front/rear axle beam. The deck/rails/axle/tongue rows use the structural "primary" criterion (governing detail).
- **Counts** are pass/marginal/fail over every pass/fail check row (stability, steering, braking and all structure rows including kingpin-strike and the three roll rows) excluding mass, cg and truck-fit; info rows (v-rollover, brake-traction) count as nothing. The operating column includes the 80 lb lightest-rider case (pitch-rear, D-1); margins are the worst of the operating, goal and light cases. "op" = 150 lb, "goal" = 200 lb.
- **Truck fit** column: "B" beside-wells, "S" straddle; two letters = removed config, tongue-off config; Y = some orientation fits. A design's **intended mode** is beside whenever its removed config fits beside the wells, otherwise straddle; it must fit in that mode with the removables off (D-9) to count as feasible. Note derived().maxTrackBesideWells = 35.50 in is the conservative bound (wheels fore-aft over the wells); the fit check accepts wider tracks beside the wells when both axles sit clear of the well span fore-aft (D-6, D-23).
- **Score** (the Pareto ordering): the minimum margin over the five stability checks (tip-straight, tip-lock, slide-before-tip, pitch-fwd, pitch-rear), worst of both riders. Mass is the tiebreak (lighter first). "All-pass" = zero fails at both operating and goal for every check, **and** the removed configuration fits the truck in the intended mode.
- Stages: (1) one-at-a-time around the defaults, (2) a track 6 x wheelbase 5 x wheel 3 x deck height 3 x lock 3 x seat 3 x pull 4 x hardware 3 = 29160 grid (nose deck raise = max(0.5, 9 - deck height), because the validator needs the nose deck underside at about 9 in for the turntable stack), (3) beside vs straddle, polished by coordinate descent. The hardware (kingpin and axle diameters with the lead and front beam width co-varied to their minimum valid values, deck ply, spacing) is a dimension of the grid with the 3 lightest hardware sets that pass every structural check at the default layout (stage 1c), one per distinct kingpin/axle pair.
- Evaluations: 28811 valid designs evaluated, 15555 skipped as validator-invalid (12384 of them in the grid). Run time 99 s. Run with `node scripts/run-ts.mjs scripts/explore.ts`.

Defaults for reference (150/200 lb): empty mass 122.2 lb, transport 110.2 lb, CG 15.1 in. Failing at default: turn-radius, pitch-rear, axle-stress, deck, kingpin-strike.

- **kingpin-strike** is almost purely kingpin diameter: 0.5 in -144%, 0.625 in -25%, 0.75 in -1%, **0.875 in +13%**, 1 in +22% (stage 1, default layout, tongue 24). Nothing else in the sweep moves it by more than a few points (pull option, wheelbase 44 vs 60: -156% vs -117%). The limit is oak bearing around the bore, so a deeper front beam or a steel sleeve would also help; neither was swept (beam depth is not a swept variable, and beam width does nothing). Bolt shear and bending are not close to governing.
- **Kingpin to rod coupling**: the lead (rod radius + pin radius + 0.5 in web, rounded up to 1/16 in) grows from 1.125 in (0.5/0.75) to 1.4375 in (1.0/0.875), the front beam must widen from 3.5 to 3.875 in, and the nose/shoulder re-derive from the larger lead. Cost on the other checks is small (about +1 lb, steer-clear +/-5 points, no turn-radius change at fixed lock because the instant center is on the axle line); a wider beam (4 in and up) is what breaks steer-clear (-16%, beam end hits the nose rail).
- **roll-kingpin-uplift** (washer bearing on the beam underside) gets *worse* with a larger pin because the washer OD default is fixed at 1.25 in: 0.625 in pin -6%, 0.75 in -25%, 1 in -125% at that washer. Co-varying the washer to 1.375-1.75 in (>= 1.3 in^2 of annulus) fixes it (+24 to +31%). So the hardware set is **pin 0.875 in (washer 1.625 in) or 1 in (washer 1.75 in)**, not the pin alone. The other roll rows (side-bearer, bolster attachment) have large margins at 150-200 lb.
- **pitch-rear** (T_lift/150 lbf >= 1.25 at 80 lb): rope bridle only +35% at the default layout; tongue 12 in +6%, 18 in -5%, 24 in -15%, 36 in -28%. Seat H-point forward helps about 3 points per inch (seat 8 in -27%, 12 in -15%, 16 in -2% with the 24 in tongue), a 0.875 in axle rod helps (-10% vs -15%, heavier front end) and wheelbase barely matters. No grid design passes with a 24 in tongue (0 of 4,194); the survivors are 73 rope-only, 67 with a 12 in tongue and 36 with an 18 in tongue.
- **turn-radius** is now to the outer tire edge: -1% at lock 38.5 / wheelbase 48 / track 35.5 (97.25 in vs 96). Wheelbase 44 gives +5%, 46 +2%, lock 39 only 0%; each 2 in of wheelbase is about 3.5 points, each 1 deg of lock about 2 points near 38 deg. Wider track costs about 0.5 point per inch.
- **axle-stress** (now with lateral load, goal rider): 0.75 in rod -3% at wheelbase 48, +1% at 44 and -11% at 60; 0.875 in rod +26% and 1 in rod +35% (groove bearing follows: 0.625 in rod fails the groove at -2%). A 0.625 in rod is out (-77%).
- **deck** (effective-width method): 9 mm needs 6 in spacing (+33%, 125.6 lb); 12 mm needs 8 in (+40%) or 10 in (+8%, marginal); 12 mm at 12 in fails (-). 6 mm and spacing 10-12 with 9 mm do not pass. The deck result depends on the crossmember count, so it jumps with wheelbase (44 in: -202%, 46: -97%, 48: -118% at 9 mm/12 in).

### Cheapest fix near the defaults

**4 changes** is the minimum (no 3-change combination passes): kingpin 0.875 in with the 0.875 in rod (lead 1.375 in, washer 1.625 in, front beam 3.75 in), 6 in crossmember spacing at 9 mm, **rope bridle only (or a 12 in tongue) and wheelbase 44 in**. Mass 125.1 lb (+3 lb over the default), transport 114.9 lb. Every row passes at operating, goal and 80 lb; the thin margins are turn-radius (+5%), kingpin-strike at goal (+8%), brake (+6%) and the turntable at goal (461 vs 500 lbf, marginal). Variants with a 12 in tongue (+16% pitch-rear) or the 18 in tongue (+2%) cost 1 lb. Why four: pin and rod pair (kingpin-strike and axle goal), deck (spacing), pitch-rear (pull), turn radius (wheelbase 44 or 46 with 12 mm).

### Grid result (all pass at operating, 150 lb goal and 80 lb light)

176 of 16,776 valid designs (of 29,160 grid points) pass every row; 169 also fit the truck in the intended mode (all of them beside the wells). 249 designs pass at operating but not at the goal rider: 243 by kingpin-strike and 68 by the turntable rating (front axle load x 3 vs 500 lbf: it bites when the seat moves forward). The failing-check census on the grid: turn-radius 13,728, kingpin-strike 12,396, steer-clear 8,592, pitch-rear 5,288, turntable 3,807, roll-kingpin-uplift 6, roll-side-bearer 2. Margins are tight everywhere: the best all-pass designs have a worst margin of only +9% to +15% over stability + turn radius + steer clearance + brake force, and the front of the stability-score ordering sits at steer-clear +1% or turn-radius +2%.

Structure-wise, no combination of kingpin 0.5 to 1 in, rod 0.625 to 1 in, ply 9/12 mm and spacing 6-12 in passes every structural row at the default layout unless it is pin >= 0.875 in with a washer >= 1.625 in and rod >= 0.875 in (15 of 152 sets; the lightest is 129.9 lb with 9 mm ply at 6 in spacing, measured at the default layout).

### Beside wells vs straddle

- **Beside**: 169 survivors. Best by the gate score is candidate A.
- **Straddle**: none among the 29,160 grid points (the grid had straddle tracks of 38 and 40 in at wheelbases 42-50). A targeted search (track 37-42, wheelbase 47-52, lock 36-40, seat 10-14, wheels 10-13 in, rope or 12 in tongue; 9,720 tried) found **23 straddle-feasible designs**, best at track 37 in, wheelbase 47 in, lock 40 deg (candidate C): 129.5 lb, kingpin-strike only +0%, turn radius +2%, tip-lock +27% (beside A: +20%), slide +42%. Straddle needs wheelbase >= 47 in (the well span; the removed cart does not straddle at 44-46) and at lock 38.5-40 deg the turn radius then runs out at wheelbase 49-50 in. It also costs +7 lb. The only gain is tip margin, which is not binding. **Beside is still the recommendation.**

### Suspicious or questionable analysis results (reported, not fixed)

1. **Washer OD does not scale with the pin.** roll-kingpin-uplift decreases (gets worse) with bigger pins at a fixed 1.25 in washer (a 1 in pin leaves 0.44 in^2); it is a model/param coupling, not a physical trend, but the default hardware silently locks the user out of the kingpin-strike fix unless the washer param is also changed. I co-varied it here. Suggest the washer default scale or a validator hint.
2. **kingpin-strike stacks two conservatisms**: V = 3 x static wheel load (dynamic factor) and H = V tan(phi) at the full 3 in curb angle (57 deg), applied to both wheels at once, plus the bearing is evaluated as a uniform bore stress. 706 lbf at the goal rider is a lot for a ~60 lb front axle load. It drives the entire hardware change (0.5 to 0.875 in pin). Worth confirming with the critic that the curb case at a scout-cart speed should include the x3 on top of tan(phi).
3. **kingpin-strike margin is bearing-governed, but the tabulated "value" mixes units**: the row shows psi against a 358 psi limit; kingpin bolt stress (130 ksi at 0.5 in) from round 1 no longer governs. Fine, but the check name suggests the bolt.
4. **Auto nose still ignores the front-beam end**: at beam width >= 4 in (needed for lead > 1.4 in) steer-clear goes to -16% (beam-F vs rail-nose). At 34.5 in track it is fine (beam 3.75-3.875 in); it breaks at track >= 36.5 in as before.
5. **Turntable rating now binds the seat-forward lever**: front axle load x 3 vs 500 lbf fails (goal) in 3,807 grid points, essentially the seat >= 14-16 in ones. pitch-rear wants the seat forward, the turntable wants it back; this is a genuine coupling but it only shows up at the goal rider, so the D-1 flag matters.
6. **Wheelbase moves deck margin non-monotonically** (44 in: -202%, 46: -97%, 48: -118% at 9 mm/12 in spacing) because the crossmember count is discrete; and spacing 10 and 12 give identical results. Expected, but means deck results must be re-checked after any wheelbase change.
7. **Axle margin falls with wheelbase** (+1% at 44, -3% at 48, -11% at 60) at the goal rider, in the rear axle. Plausible because the rear wheel load grows with the longer lever, but worth a hand check against the new lateral-load term.
8. **Brake lever ratio is still free** (mass and geometry do not change with 6:1 to 14:1).
9. **Candidate A uses wheelbase 40 in and seat 8 in** because the gate score rewards short wheelbases (turn radius) and the validator allows it; the rider sits far back (pitch-rear +19%, pitch-forward huge). It is a valid optimum of the stated objective but not necessarily a sensible cart; candidate B (4 changes) is the one to start from.
10. **Mass**: every design that passes sits at about 121-135 lb empty; the 60-70 lb target remains out of reach (D-21).

### Recommended starting point

Candidate B (cheapest fix): kingpin 0.875 in, rod 0.875 in, washer 1.625 in, lead 1.375 in, front beam 3.75 in, 9 mm deck at 6 in spacing, rope bridle only (12 in tongue also passes), wheelbase 44 in, everything else default. Thin margins: turn-radius +5%, kingpin-strike at goal +8%, brake +6% (raise the ratio to 12:1). If Eric wants more breathing room on the pin, use the 1 in pin (+19%, one 1/16 in lead step).
