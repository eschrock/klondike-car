# Phase 2 parameter-space exploration

Generated by `scripts/explore.ts` (see Method). Numbers below are reproducible; the Observations section is hand-written and preserved on re-runs.

## Method

- Everything runs with **autoNoseGeometry on** (D-26): nose width and shoulder are derived per design from front track, lock and lead, so the Phase 1 coupling (C1) does not cap the sweep. All designs go through `validateParams`; designs with a validator **error** are skipped and counted ("invalid").
- Each design is evaluated with `runChecks` (resolved params, goal-rider model, both rider cases) plus `checkTruckFit` (all modes and configs, 0.25 in search step) computed once and reused.
- **Margins** are fractional: (value - limit)/limit for ">=" checks, (limit - value)/limit for "<=" checks, taken at the **worse of the 150 lb operating and 200 lb goal case**. Negative (bold) means a fail in at least one case; under +10% is "marginal". pitch-fwd has a limit of 0 deg, so its margin is degrees of slope headroom divided by the 15 deg design slope. v-roll is info only (A-1), shown for reference. beams = worse of front/rear axle beam. The deck/rails/axle/tongue rows use the structural "primary" criterion (governing detail).
- **Counts** are pass/marginal/fail over the 19 pass/fail-or-reported checks excluding mass, cg and truck-fit; info rows (v-rollover, brake-traction, bearing...) count as nothing. "op" = 150 lb, "goal" = 200 lb.
- **Truck fit** column: "B" beside-wells, "S" straddle; two letters = removed config, tongue-off config; Y = some orientation fits. A design's **intended mode** is beside whenever its removed config fits beside the wells, otherwise straddle; it must fit in that mode with the removables off (D-9) to count as feasible. Note derived().maxTrackBesideWells = 35.50 in is the conservative bound (wheels fore-aft over the wells); the fit check accepts wider tracks beside the wells when both axles sit clear of the well span fore-aft (D-6, D-23).
- **Score** (the Pareto ordering): the minimum margin over the five stability checks (tip-straight, tip-lock, slide-before-tip, pitch-fwd, pitch-rear), worst of both riders. Mass is the tiebreak (lighter first). "All-pass" = zero fails at both operating and goal for every check, **and** the removed configuration fits the truck in the intended mode.
- Stages: (1) one-at-a-time around the defaults, (2) a track 8 x wheelbase 5 x wheel 3 x deck height 4 x lock 3 x seat 4 x pull 5 = 28800 grid (nose deck raise = max(0.5, 9 - deck height), because the validator needs the nose deck underside at about 9 in for the turntable stack), (3) beside vs straddle, polished by coordinate descent. The deck ply and crossmember spacing are fixed in stage 2 at the lightest combination that clears the deck check by >= 10% (9 mm, 6 in spacing) because the deck check depends on nothing else.
- Evaluations: 24103 valid designs evaluated, 5314 skipped as validator-invalid (5240 of them in the grid). Run time 85 s. Run with `node scripts/run-ts.mjs scripts/explore.ts`.

Defaults for reference (150/200 lb): empty mass 122.2 lb, transport 110.2 lb, CG 15.1 in. Failing at default: pitch-rear, deck.

### What drives what

- **Defaults fail two checks** (D-1 both riders): **pitch-rear** (23.7 deg vs 30 deg, -21%) and **deck** (0.092 in vs 0.032 in; the governing criterion is the standing-scout deflection/bending on the largest crossmember span). Turn radius (+1%), brake force (+6%) and steer clearance (+10%) are marginal. Everything structural except the deck has large margins (beams +86%, rails +37%, axle +34%, tongue +14%).
- **Deck**: purely ply thickness and joist spacing, independent of the layout. 9 mm needs 6 in spacing (+26%, +3.4 lb); 12 mm needs 8 in (+27%, +4.4 lb); 18 mm clears at the default spacing (+53%, +9 lb). 6 mm never passes. Spacing 10 and 12 in give identical results (the crossmember count is discrete), and 14 in drops a joist and gets worse. The 9 mm / 6 in answer is the lightest fix, but see "suspicious" below on how conservative the deck model is.
- **pitch-rear (climbing with the 150 lbf design pull)** is the dominant stability limiter, and it is set by **pull attachment and seat position**, not by the track or wheelbase:
  - rope-bridle only: +8% at default (pull point at the front beam); a wood tongue costs about 8 points per 6 in of length (12 in: -5%, 24 in: -21%, 48 in: -53%); every 2 in the seat H-point moves forward buys about 10 points (seat 16 in with the 24 in tongue is still -2%).
  - In the 28,800-point grid the all-pass designs are 207 rope-only, 57 with a 12 in tongue, 13 with an 18 in tongue and none with a 24 in or 36 in tongue. So a tongue longer than about 18 in cannot pass at all with this layout; the tongue also fails its own bending check beyond about 28 in (30 in: -10%).
  - Smaller wheels make it worse (10 in: -31% vs 13 in: -21% at default).
- **Turn radius** is set by lock, wheelbase and track together (turntable steering: radius = front-axle distance to the instant centre + half the front track). At the 39 deg end of the lock range, wheelbase <= 48 in is needed at 35.5 in track (50 in: -3%, 60 in: -19%), and track costs about 0.5 point per inch. Lock below 36 deg fails it at wheelbase 48 (30 deg: -19%). Wider lock is capped by the nose width the rider's boots need (the validator error "nose is # wide inside" accounts for 1218 skipped designs).
- **steer-clear** is coupled to track, wheel size and deck height: the resolveParams nose width (auto, D-26) is sized from the tire swing only. At track >= 36.5 in, at 11.5 in wheels (-13%) and at deck height 8.5 in (tongue vs front transom, 0.38 in) the limiter is something else: the **front axle beam end vs the nose rail** (beam-F / rail-nose-L, 0.59 in at 38 in track, 0.61 in with 11.5 in wheels), or the tongue vs the front transom. Auto nose does not size against those, so they need a rule of their own.
- **Lateral stability** (tip-straight +20%, tip-lock +27%, slide-before-tip +42% at default) is never the binding constraint. It improves roughly +1.7 points (tip) and +4 points (slide) per inch of track (35.5 to 50 in: tip-straight +20% to +45%) and degrades by about 3 points of tip margin per inch of deck height. v-rollover (info, A-1) sits at 9 mph +-1: it is below 1.5 x jog speed for deck height >= 9.5 in or wheelbase < 46 in.
- **Wheel diameter**: 13 in is best for pitch-rear (-21% vs -31% at 10 in, default layout); 10 in saves ~2 lb; 11.5 in is a trap with the auto nose at default (beam-end clearance, -13%), though it appears in good grid designs at narrow track.
- **Deck height**: lower is better for lateral stability and mass (about 2 lb per inch), but below 9 in the nose deck must be raised to keep >= 9 in over the turntable stack (validator), and below about 7.25 in the rear axle beam + ply no longer fit under the deck (validator). 8-8.5 in with a 0.5-1 in nose raise is the sweet spot. Deck height does not change turn radius or brake force.
- **Brake**: brake-force depends only on the lever ratio (6:1 -56%, 8:1 -17%, 10:1 +6%, 12:1 +22%, 14:1 +33%) and on the rider mass; it does not change mass or any geometry in the model.
- **Goal rider**: the 200 lb goal rider only makes brake force marginal. No design in the grid passes at 150 lb and fails at 200 lb (operating-only passes: 0), so the goal rider costs nothing in this envelope beyond thinner brake margin.

### Coupled constraints (validator)

Of 29,445 designs tried, 5,323 were validator-invalid. The reasons, in order: the **seat cushion runs into the cabin shoulder** (seat forward + wide track, because auto shoulder moves back as the swing grows: track 42 with seat 17 fails), the **cabin rails reach past the rear U-bolt plates** (narrow track at 28 in cabin width; track below about 34 in needs a narrower cabin), the **foot stop runs into the front transom** (short wheelbase + forward seat), and the **nose too narrow for the rider's feet** (narrow track with a high lock, because the auto nose follows the swing). Practically: the seat-forward fix for pitch-rear (seat 14 to 17 in) conflicts with wide track, so straddle designs at track >= 40 in need the seat at about 14 in or less (track 42 with seat 17 is invalid, with seat 14 it is valid).

### Beside wells vs straddle

- Beside is feasible up to about 36.5 in track (the fit check passes the removed cart beside the wells when both axles sit fore-aft clear of the wells, which is more than derived().maxTrackBesideWells = 35.5 in), and at wheelbase 44-48 in. Straddle needs a longer wheelbase: at the default layout the removed cart does not straddle at 44-46 in and does from 48 in; at the straddle candidate it fits from 47 in up to 60 in. With lock 39 deg and track 37 in the turn-radius limit leaves only 47-48 in (49 in: -1%).
- The two modes reach nearly the same lateral stability once pitch-rear is solved with the seat: the straddle design (track 37, wheelbase 47) gains about +5 points of tip-lock and +8 points of slide margin over the best beside design, but pays for it with ~7 lb (the beam and deck widen), a 4 in longer wheelbase (turn radius +3% vs +10% beside), and the validator coupling above. Beside at 34.5 in track is lighter (117.5 lb vs 124.3 lb) and has >= +18% on every tip and structural check; its weak spots are pitch-rear (+9%, marginal) and brake force (+8%).
- **Recommendation**: stay with beside-wells stowage (candidate A). Straddle only buys tip margin that is not the binding constraint.

### Surprises and suspicious analysis results (not fixed; report only)

1. **Deck check is very conservative.** The default 9 mm okoume deck at 10-12 in joist spacing fails by -189% (utilization 2.9) under a 200 lb standing scout; a 12 in strip, simply supported, with no plate action. Real 9 mm marine ply on 12 in joists carries a standing adult. The fix it implies (6 in joists or 18 mm ply, +3 to +9 lb) is probably an over-correction; the critic should confirm the 4 in x 10 in patch and the 12 in strip assumption.
2. **Brake lever ratio is free**: ratio 6:1 to 14:1 changes no mass or geometry, so the optimiser would always take 14:1 (+33%). It needs a geometry/mass cost (lever length vs the 30 lbf handle stroke) or a cap, or the exploration should treat brake as satisfied at the maximum ratio.
3. **steer-clear is non-monotonic**: with auto nose quantised to 1/8 in, adjacent lock values move steer-clearance by +-5 points (lock 36: +9%, 37: +15%, 38: +11%, 38.5: +10%). Real, but it means a design that looks marginal can jump to pass by a 1 degree change. Also the limiter changes between the tire, the beam end, the tongue and the transom (see above).
4. **Auto nose geometry vs beam end**: see steer-clear. Either resolveParams should include the beam-F end (and the clamp plates), or the validator should warn.
5. **Mass**: the entire explored envelope spans only 118-138 lb empty (transport 107-127 lb). Deck ply, joist spacing, track and deck height are the only meaningful mass levers (about 0.9 lb per inch of track, 2 lb per inch of deck height; 3-9 lb for the deck fix). D-21's 60-70 lb target is not reachable from these variables; the lightweight variant needs structural changes (open frame, 10 in wheels without a rigid box) not in this sweep.
6. **pitch-rear versus track**: wider track gently improves pitch-rear (35.5 in: -21%; 50 in: -9%) though the check is about fore-aft balance; presumably the wider beams and deck add mass in front of the CG. Plausible, but worth a look.
7. **Truck-fit non-monotonic in wheelbase**: at the straddle candidate the removed config fits at every wheelbase 47-60 in, but the tongue-off config fits only at 47 in and 50-52 in (the tires land on the hinge gap or the well span at other fore-aft positions). Real geometry (D-23), but fit has to be re-checked after any wheelbase change and the tongue-off window is not a single interval.
8. **v-rollover** swings from -20% to +20% across the wheelbase and track sweeps with no pass/fail consequence (A-1); at the best designs it is within 5% of 1.5 x jog speed.
9. Brake-traction (info, D-24) is about 1.0 x on 15 deg nose-down parked at the best designs (0.94-1.05); not a driver.

### Recommended starting point

Candidate A below: beside the wells, 34.5 in track, 43 in wheelbase, 11.5 in wheels, deck height 8.5 in, seat H-point 13 in, lock 39 deg, 9 mm deck ply at 6 in joist spacing, **rope bridle only**. Raise the brake ratio to 12:1 (+22% instead of +8%). Pitch-rear (+9%), turn radius (+10%) and steer clearance (+11%) are the thin margins; do not exceed 39 deg lock. 11.5 in wheels are sensitive to the beam-end clearance (0.77 in at 34.5 in track, fails at default 35.5 in), so 13 in wheels (candidate C family) are the safer alternative if wheel availability matters. A wood tongue is possible only up to about 12 in with the seat forward (candidate C) and leaves every pull-related margin at about +6%.
