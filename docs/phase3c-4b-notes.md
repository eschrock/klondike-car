# Phase 3c-4b notes: character and ergonomics (D-57)

Scope is D-57 items 1 to 10 (the sheer and plan turn-in, 14 in rear, bow bulkhead as the foot brace and lift hand-hold, flag tube, hinged seat back, parked handlebow, grab boards, step brake, sign, bandsawn handlebow). The step brake (item 8) was built by a sub-agent: its numbers are in section 8. The front axle (pivot, tongue, stops, front analysis, rear beam seat) is 3c-4a's. `DECISIONS.md` and `docs/STATUS.md` are not touched; proposed decision text is at the end. Nothing is committed.

## 1. Sheer and plan curve (items 1, 2)

- `sideHeightRearIn` 14. `sheerCurveIn` (new, replaces `sideProfileSagIn`, slider, default 2.5): the gunwale top dips this far below the straight rear-to-bow line at mid-length (a parabola). With the 10 in of rise, 2.5 in makes the top level at the bow and steepest (20 degrees) at the rear: heights above the deck at x = -9.25 / 0 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 49 are 14 / 11.1 / 8.5 / 6.5 / 5.1 / 4.2 / 4.0 in. Range 0 (the old straight line) to 4.
- `bowTurnInIn` 1.25 (slider) and `bowTurnLenIn` 14: a smoothstep (zero slope at both ends) from x = 35 to the bulkhead face at x = 49. `bowTurnInAt(p, x)` in `params.ts`, `Layout.turnIn(x)`, `innerHalfWidth(L, x)` and `outerHalfWidth(L, x)` read it. Outer half width at x = 38 / 40 / 42 / 44 / 46 / 48 / 49: 11.85 / 11.64 / 11.38 / 11.11 / 10.90 / 10.77 / 10.75 in.
- The 24 in body is unchanged behind x = 35. The bulkhead is 21.5 in wide.
- **Construction.** The chine, gunwale, web, front rails and deck follow the curves (all five are `plan-wall.ts` meshes or a sampled extrusion; the overlap tests cover them with 1.25 in slabs, `Geometry.mesh.slabs`). **Chine and gunwale: bent-laminated.** Two 5/16 in white-oak plies are cold-bent flat on one plan form (the bend is across the 5/8 in thickness; the tightest radius is 6 x 14 -> L^2/(6 d) = 26 in, against a limit of 40 x 5/16 = 12.5 in, `validateParams`), epoxied, then the gunwale is **bandsawn to the sheer** (a blank about 11 in wide). The chine stays a constant 1.5 in band. The web is one 6 mm okoume sheet cut to the sheer pattern and bent flat in plan. The rails get the same plan bend (2 plies), the step and notches cut after bending. No exposed ply edge (variants test still enforces it).
- Joinery follows: web ends in a stopped dado in the bulkhead, chine, gunwale and rail ends Domino or loose tenon into its rear face, deck edge in a bulkhead dado. Rear transom unchanged.
- Validator: bulkhead high enough for the hand-hold, bend radius, nose width, feet reach, stop position vs the rail inner face at the end joist.

## 2. Bow bulkhead, foot brace, lift hand-hold, foot block (item 3)

- `bow-bulkhead` (group body, oak, `bowBulkheadThickIn` 1.75, `bowBulkheadHeightIn` 9). The D-54 curl and its params (`bowCurl*`) are gone; `bowCurl(p)` stays only as an alias of `bowBulkhead(p)` so `steerGeometry` (3c-4a) still reads `xFlatEnd`. **`frontOverhangIn` 12 -> 6.75** so the end of the flat deck (x = 49.0, was 49.1) and so the end joist, stops and king beam did not move; the nose is 5 in shorter (bow tip x 50.75, tongue tip 4 in past it).
- Its rear face is the foot brace: `seatXIn + riderLegReachIn = 13.5 + 35.5 = 49 = Layout.bulkhead.x0` (validated). Foot-stop row (`hull.ts`, kept id): bulkhead plank bending (span 20.25 in, 3 in band after the hand-hold slot, 1.75 in thick) + the pull-off checks (deck-dado glue, rail/chine/gunwale tenons) + the hard-hit case, or the foot block and its slot walls when a block is in. Goal rider at 3 mph: 0.10 util (pass), 585 to 650 psi.
- **Lift hand-hold** (published for 3c-4a's `lift` row): `Layout.hold = { x0, x1, halfLen, zBot, zTop, zMid }` (the slot's x range is the bulkhead's thickness, 4.5 x 1.25 in, bottom 3 in above the deck, 4.75 in of oak above it at the crown) and `bowHold(ctx)` in `body.ts` returns it in the `LiftSlot` shape `liftBarStressPsi` takes (zBot, zTop, barIn, thickIn, lengthIn). The part id is `bow-bulkhead`; it is in the `removed` configuration (it is structural). The old front lifter's rope loop at the tongue is no longer the only front hold. (3c-4a already switched `lift`; at the defaults it passes at 0.53.)
- **Drop-in foot block.** Two oak slot cleats per side (`foot-slot-L1/L2/R1/R2`, 0.75 in thick, 3.5 in long, 4 in tall, a 1-1/2 in slot open at the top, 1 in walls) at 4 and 8 in behind the bulkhead face (`footBlockStepIn`, `footBlockPositions`). New param `footBlockSlot` (0 out by default, 1, 2) shows the `foot-block` (1.5 x 4 in oak board, removable) in a slot; set `riderLegReachIn` to match a shorter rider. The cleats stand 4 in proud inside the sides (they narrow the bed by 3/4 in a side there: noted in the part).

## 3. Flag tube (item 4)

The printed bow flag clip and socket are gone. `flag-tube`: a purchased 1 in schedule 40 tube (1.05 in ID, 1.315 in OD, `flagTubeIdIn`, `flagTubeOdIn`) standing on the deck in the **left rear corner**, against the front face of the handle-socket backer, top flush with the gunwale top at that x (z = 22), with a 3/16 in drain hole above the deck (the water leaves through the rear scupper). Two oak holding blocks (`flag-block-top`, `flag-block-bottom`, 3/4 x 1.4 x 1.5 in) are screwed to the side. **To clear the lid's swing the tube stands beside the box, not over it**, so `storageSideClearIn` went from 1/8 to 1.5 in (a gutter on both sides; the box is 19.75 in wide). Lash plates keep clear of it and of the grab boards, and stay on the straight part of the side (x < 33).

## 4. Seat back hinges and flips flat (item 5)

- **Forward onto the deck** (picked over back onto the lid): the lid stays free to open, the bed is 16 in longer flat, and the box lid height (7.5 in) would put the back's base far from the cushion level if it flipped aft.
- `seat-hinge-L/R` (316 butt hinges, knuckle at the stile's front foot P, along y) screw to the back's front face and through the 12 mm deck into `seat-hinge-backer` (oak under the deck, found between joists by `hingeBacker`). Upright, it leans 15 degrees onto two **fixed** oak stop blocks (`seat-wedge-L/R`, glued to the deck and the box end, no longer loose). One 3/8 in quick-release (ball-lock) pin per stile (`seat-pin-L/R`, lanyard) through a stile hole at `seatBackLatchHeightIn` (3 in) into a blind hole in the stop block holds it **up**; the same pins through the same holes down through the stiles into the deck and its backer hold it **flat**. No tools, both positions. The knobs, inserts, backers and stud plugs are gone.
- **Flat pose** (`ViewState.seat = 'stowed'`, the toolbar box is now "Seat flat (cargo mode)"): rotated 90 degrees + the recline about the hinge, front face on the deck; lies from x = 9.5 to 25.5, 3/4 in proud, 24 in short of the foot brace. The pad (Dual Lock, now removable) and the seat cushion lie in the box.
- **Box.** It holds the cushions in cargo mode (`seat-stow`: need 14.38 x 18.25 x 3.38 in against 14.80 x 19.28 x 6.75 in, margins 0.42 / 1.03 / 3.37 in: pass) **or** the 10 Essentials in race mode (`storage-pack` fill 0.83: pass). **Both together do not fit** (the row says so and gives the volumes: cushions 646 in³, Essentials about 1,700 in³ with the packing allowance, 1,279 in³ free after the cushions): carry the Essentials in a pack on the bed in cargo mode, or raise `storageVolumeIn3` to about 2,400. Default 2,000 in³ (was 2,500), interior 14.8 x 19.3 in, 7.5 in tall outside.
- **Rider room restored.** Seat H-point x 13.5 (was 16), leg reach 35.5 in (was 32), cushion depth 14.5 (was 16) so the cushion fits the box with the seat that far back. Limiting dimension: the box must be about 14.5 in long inside for the cushion and its end leans back with the seat back (the back's rear face at the lid height sets the box end).
- Rows: `seat-back` (stile bending, crest bearing on the stop block, hinge load per hinge, panel; goal rider util 0.48), `seat-stow` (above), `storage-lid-open` (75 degrees upright).

## 5. Handlebow (items 6, 10)

- `handleBowCrownIn` 7/8 (was 3). The bow is **one bandsawn board** (`handle-bar`, extrusion in y-z, 5/4 white oak, 1.25 in thick, about 3 in wide, 24 in long). Grain runs out 10 to 12 degrees at the posts and ends (level at the middle where the pull is): `handle-bow` row now has no lamination factor, a Wood Handbook slope-of-grain factor (0.45 at 1:5) at the overhanging ends; util 0.15.
- **Parked position.** `ViewState.handle = 'up' | 'parked'` (toolbar "Handle parked"). Sockets 16 in deep (`handleSocketDepthIn` 6 -> 16): the pin is 1.5 in below the socket mouth; riding, the post stands 5.7 in in the socket; parked, the posts and the bow sink **9.8 in** (`handleParkClearIn` 1 in clearance over the transom top, `parkDropIn`) so the bow rests at z 24.2 above the 22 in transom corners (22.75 at its crown). The post has two cross holes 9.8 in apart. **The 6 in sockets would have been too shallow** (6 in could not take a 9.8 in drop with 5 in of insertion); each 16 in aluminum tube is 10 in longer than before (about +0.7 lb each; handle-sockets row util 0.30, the longer insertion helps). Alternative if the long socket is not wanted: lift the handle out and lay it on the box lid (the bow is 24 in, the lid 15 x 19.7).

## 6. Grab boards (item 7)

## 8. Step brake (item 8)

Built by a sub-agent (`src/model/brake-layout.ts`, `brake.ts`, `src/analysis/brake.ts`, 70 tests passing). The D-55 steel drag bar, arms, shoe beam, latch and toe guard and 29 params are deleted (old URLs still load; the old keys are ignored).

- **Geometry (per side, mirrored, every y follows `rearTrackIn / 2`):** a 1 x 3.5 in oak flap over the tire; a 316 butt hinge at x = 7.0 (ahead of the tire, which is why the sign stops at x = 6) on an oak knee/outrigger bolted through the rail with 4 x 3/8 in 316 bolts into a 1.25 in oak backer glued inside the rail and under the deck (a joist that meets the backer is cut back 1.25 in: small edit in `frame.ts`). The flap slopes down 6 degrees to a 5 in foot area just behind the transom face (ends at x = -13, outboard of the body, foot pad 10.8 in above the ground). A rubber pad (3/8 in) on an oak shoe block sits 6 degrees behind the top of the tire, 3/8 in off the tread when released. Return: a bungee and stop cord to an eye screw in the transom's aft face. **Chocks:** two oak wedges (28 degree ramp) parked ahead of the rear tires, each on a 1/4 in cord from an eye in the knee underside; removable.
- **Lever ratio, derived from the geometry: 2.50:1 rolling forward, 2.06:1 backward** (critical).
- **Foot force to HOLD the loaded cart on 15 degrees** (both feet, pad mu 0.25 fouled, worse rolling direction): **85 lbf at the 150 lb rider (+25 lb Essentials), 98 lbf at 200 lb, 104 lbf with the 250 lb cargo**; at the wet pad (mu 0.40) the 150 lb case needs 56 lbf. Limit `brakeFootForceLimitLbf` = 90 lbf (a 180 lb handler standing on both flaps; it was 60 for the D-55 bar): `brake-force` is marginal at 150, **fails at 200 and cargo, truthfully**. 90 is a judgment: it is one param.
- **Slows and stops** (`brake-slow`, pass at all three cases): at the 90 lbf limit the flaps hold up to 15.9 degrees (150 lb), 13.7 (200 lb), 12.8 (cargo), above the 10 degree stop grade (`brakeStopSlopeDeg`); on one foot only 7.9 degrees.
- **Park / chock** (`park-hold`): at ground mu 0.60 the chock holds with 1.13x margin at 150 and 200 lb; on wet or fouled ground (mu 0.40) the wooden chock slides (0.75x), flagged in the row but not its status; the cargo case is info (the forward payload unloads the rear tires, 0.35x). A single chock cannot hold the slope. The cord only tethers the chock.
- **Other rows:** `brake-ratio`, `brake-traction` (info), `brake-clear` (closest approach 0.22 in), `brake-flap`, `brake-hinge`, `brake-mount`, `brake-return`, `brake-abuse` (all pass), `handle-sockets` unchanged (pass). `brake-lever`, `-links`, `-beam`, `-shoes` deleted. `park-hold` moved to Braking and to the Hills light.
- **Mass:** brake group 13.2 lb including the handle sockets (about 9.4 lb at HEAD), 1.2 lb of it the removable chocks and cords.
- **Consequence for the Simple view:** the Hills light goes red above about the 150 lb rider (hold force above 90 lbf) and the 200 lb goal rider is flagged under D-1. That follows from the numbers.
- **Decisions to record:** the 90 lbf limit, pad mu 0.40 / 0.25, chock mu 0.60 / 0.40, hinge at x = 7.0, knee on the rail with a backer. A smooth wooden chock under a free-rolling wheel will not hold 15 degrees on slick ground whatever its ramp angle.
