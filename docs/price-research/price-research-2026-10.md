# Price research, October 2026

Accessed 2026-10-12. Base prices for the non-lumber lines of the BOM (`pkg/bom.csv`) against the estimates in `src/build/prices.ts`. Nothing was bought. Lumber and plywood were skipped (Eric's quotes). Machine-readable twin: `docs/price-research/price-research-2026-10.json`.

## How to read the evidence

- **[F]** the page was fetched and the price read from it. **[S]** the price came from the search tool's reading of the vendor page (a snippet, not fetched by me); these are less certain, and two Bolt Depot pages disagreed with each other on a few prices. **[L]** manufacturer list.
- Blocked or unreadable: Jamestown Distributors (page returns empty), Home Depot, Lowe's, Defender, Bolt Depot (direct fetch 403; read via search), eBay, Amazon, Zoro (403), West Marine (404), McMaster-Carr (specs readable, prices rendered by JavaScript). Not attempted: Fastenal, Northern Tool, Rockler product pages. No guessed number is presented as found.
- "Recommended" means the cheapest credible price for the exact spec. A substitute is labelled as such and is never marked recommended unless it is the only option.

## Coverage

68 research lines (one per BOM item or item group): **46 found** at the right spec, **11 found only as a substitute or with a spec gap** (wrong bore, 304 not 316, thicker bar, larger reflector), **1 blocked / price not readable**, **10 not found**.

Counts are by research line, so the wheel line counts as found even though the McMaster wheel price is hidden. Not found or blocked, for Eric to quote by hand: 3/4"-bore wheel at McMaster (8353T33, spec matches, price hidden); 1/4 x 1-3/4 and 1/16 x 1 316 bar; 1/4" UHMW; 5/8 x 1 x 1-1/2 bronze sleeve; 2-1/2" 316 hinge; 316 draw latch; 316 4-hole D-ring plate; 3/8" detent pin; 316 clevis pin and cotter pin; #10 316 screw eye; 316 threaded inserts; Sipo 4x20 Dominos (none exist); exterior deck screws; US price for ASA filament.

## Findings that change the plan, not just the price

1. **Wheels: almost every 10" hand-truck wheel is 5/8" bore.** Harbor Freight (9.99, 300 lb, 3 lb), Tractor Supply/Martin (17.99), Uline (34) and CasterHQ (71) are all 5/8". The only priced 3/4"-bore pneumatic found is Access Casters 10DHTPW-53 on eBay at 29.60 (350 lb, listed 5 lb, hub "2-3/4"). McMaster 8353T33 (350 lb, 2-1/4" side hub, 5/8" or 3/4" bore, tubeless) matches best but its price is hidden. Measure first, as the model note says.
2. **West System has no 301 or 303 pump sets any more.** The 300 Mini Pump Set (3 pumps: resin, 205/206 hardener, 207/209 hardener) does both ratios: 28.99 replaces the 18 + 20 lines.
3. **West System 207 has no quart or pint**: 207-SA is 0.66 pt (10.6 fl oz), 207-SB is 0.33 gal (42 fl oz). 209 has the same SA/SB sizes. From the finishing quantities (58.56 fl oz mixed at 3:1 for the coats, 20.17 fl oz for the closes) the hardener needed is about 14.6 fl oz of 207 and 5 fl oz of 209: one 207-SB and one 209-SA.
4. **Dominos are priced by the pack**: Sipo comes in 300 (5x30) and 100 (8x50) at 57 each. There is no Sipo 4x20. The BOM's 36 pieces cost 12.60 in the estimate and 114 as bought.
5. **316 forged shoulder eye bolts are 4x the galvanized price** (48-51 against 11-12 each at Bolt Depot). The BOM allows either; galvanized is the base price here. 3-7/8" and 2-5/8" are not stock lengths; the nearest are 4-1/2" and 2-1/2".
6. **316 stainless angle and aluminum tube cost the short-cut price**, not the stick price: Online Metals cuts 12" of angle at 19.83.
7. **A 316 3/4" set-screw collar is 33 each** (MROSupply); a 304 one is 4.30. Four collars: 133 against 24.
