# Price research, Standard weather spec, October 2026

Accessed 2026-10-12. Prices for the **Standard** weather spec (garage-kept, used a few times a year, rained on at most): 304 / 18-8 stainless, zinc or plated steel indoors, beech Dominos, Titebond III, spar urethane, and a smaller epoxy kit. The current spec (316, Sipo, marine varnish, gallon epoxy) is the **Extreme weather** option and is in `docs/price-research/price-research-2026-10.md`. Nothing was bought. Machine-readable twin: `docs/price-research/price-research-standard-2026-10.json` (same shape as the earlier JSON, plus `spec: "standard"`).

## How to read the evidence

- **[F]** the page was fetched and the price read from it. **[S]** the price came from the search tool's reading of the vendor page (a snippet, not fetched by me; Bolt Depot, Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace, Walmart and Rockler all refuse or fail the fetch tool). **[L]** manufacturer list.
- **Blocked or unreadable:** Bolt Depot (403 to the fetch tool; read through search snippets), Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace, Fastenal (403), Walmart (bot check), Rockler product pages ("header overflow"), Amazon (not attempted, no readable result), McMaster-Carr (prices rendered by JavaScript; not attempted), Everbilt/Hillman pages (Home Depot). Where a big-box pack is listed it is from a search snippet only. No price is guessed; missing items are listed as NOT FOUND.
- "Recommended" is the cheapest credible price for the Standard spec. A substitute is labelled SUBSTITUTE / spec gap.
- Bolt Depot sells by the piece at a per-piece price and also by the bag (25, 50, 100); at Eric's small counts the per-piece price is the one that applies, and it is what the table lists. Shipping and tax are not included anywhere.

## Coverage

63 research lines: **50 found** at the right spec, **8 found only as a substitute or with a spec gap** (3/8 x 6-1/4 carriage bolt as 6", thumb screw as a zinc spade 3/4", cotter pin 1-1/4" for 1-1/2", clevis pin 2" for 3.74", screw eye trade size 8, snap hook, the 2-1/2" x 2-3/4" hinge as 1-5/8 x 2-1/2, the 4-hole D-ring plate as a 2-hole zinc ring), **5 not found**: 18-8 #6 x 2-1/2", 18-8 #6 x 3-1/2", 18-8 #8 x 3/8", and the #10-32 threaded inserts in 3/8" and 5/16" lengths (a zinc softwood #10-32 insert at 0.25 is the only substitute).
