## Proposed DECISIONS.md entries (D-35 … D-37)

| ID | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| D-35 | **Captured-ply hull, real joinery.** The okoume web enters a **3/8" × (ply + 1/64") groove** in the chine (top face) and the gunwale (underside), leaving ≥ 0.1" of oak each side (validator warns below that). The decks run **1/4" into a rabbet in each chine and a dado in each transom**; the web ends enter **stopped 1/4" dados** in the transoms. The transoms are now **full width** (outside width, 1/2" thick, wrapping the side ends), so the sides run between the transom faces. Edges that cannot be captured (the deck step edges, the web ends at the shoulder, the shoulder panels, the signs) get **1/4"-class oak banding** (`edgeBandIn`, 3/16"); every ply part sets `edges` with no `exposed` entry, and `hull.test.ts` fails on any. **Joint rule by stock thickness:** a Festool Domino where the host keeps a wall ≥ 0.15" behind the mortise (3/4" crossmember into a 3/4" rail: DF 500 5×30); a **CNC blind tenon** (1/3 of the stock, 3/8" long, ≥ 1/8" wall) where it does not (1/2" transoms and hull girders: DF 500 4×20 leaves only 0.106"); glue + epoxy fillets elsewhere; threaded inserts + knobs for anything removable. XL (DF 700) is selected automatically for thick stock (≥ 2.5") but nothing at the current 1/2"–1-3/4" sizes needs it. Joints are stored on **both** mated parts with `at` in each part's local frame; `joinerySummary()` counts each once. The flared (D-6) groove follows the tilt of the web. | 🔶 |
| D-36 | **Bent-lam prow.** A `sweep` of **6 × 1/8" white-oak strips (3/4" thick) × 1-1/2" wide**: a 2.5" straight leg against the front face of the front transom (above the lift slot), then a Bezier to **18" above the gunwale line, 7-3/4" ahead of the transom**. Tightest radius 23" against a limit of 40 × strip = 5", so it lays up cold; the model flags a tighter shape. It is **removable (D-9)**: two 1/4-20 brass inserts in the leg take thumb-knob bolts through the transom (knobs inside the hull). Mass 0.75 lb (the Phase 1 block stem was 1.0 lb). The **form** is a `kind: 'reference'`, `fab: 'cnc'`, `payload: true` part (`prow-form`, shown beside the cart, 0 lb): an `extrusion`-ready CCW profile of the inside face, **overbent by a 6% spring-back allowance** (placeholder: bend a test strip first) with a backing body, in 3/4" MDF sheets. | 🔶 |
| D-37 | **Lift hand-holds (M9) and finger guards (M5).** **Lift:** a 4-1/2" × 1-1/4" stadium slot through each transom (gloved hand), bottom ≥ 3" above the deck and above a storage box that abuts the rear transom, **3/4" of oak left above it**. Rating: one hand carrying the whole per-person lift (empty transport mass / 2 = 62 lb) × 3 = 186 lbf on the bar over the slot, simply supported: **4.5 ksi against the 6.5 ksi design MOR**. The rear transom rises into a 1/4" crest to keep that bar when the box forces the slot up; the front transom keeps its top at the gunwale line (the lantern and flag clips straddle it, D-41) and lowers the slot instead. **Finger guard:** at the defaults the tire comes within **0.76"** of the hull at lock, so each side gets a printed ASA joggled-L guard: a hood flange **0.75" above the tire top** (the steering-clearance rule, so the sweep check still passes), covering the plan zone where the tire is within the no-pinch gap (1.5") of the hull at any steer angle (x 26.4–31.6", out to 5.5" from the nose side), a leg in the web recess, and a strap screwed to two #10-32 inserts in the nose gunwale. It shields the pocket from above and from behind (the shoulder); the open forward side of the sweep is not closed. The gap is recomputed from the tire plan rectangle over the whole sweep; No guard is built when the gap already meets `fingerGapMinIn` (the test patches it to 0.5" to cover that branch). | 🔶 |

## Parts built or changed

- **Decks:** `deck-cabin`, `deck-nose` are plan `extrusion`s (rabbet/dado extensions included). `deck-nose` takes the kingpin-access opening from workstream A's `Layout.accessPanel` (M10): a CW hole of the panel's plan size; the rim is banded with oak and listed in `edges` after the four outline edges (the `edges` array is outline edges first, then each hole's edges). Without the crossbar `accessPanel` is null and the deck is whole.
- **Sides (captured ply, default):** chine and gunwale boxes (same section and y/z extents as before, so the analysis reads them unchanged; x ranges now run between the full-width transoms), `*-web` extrusions (tilted with the flare), end conditions per edge.
- **Shoulder panels:** all four edges banded.
- **Transoms:** `transom-rear`, `transom-front` are full-width `extrusion`s with the slot as a hole; the rear polygon follows the flare.
- **Prow** (sweep) and **prow-form** (reference); **finger guards** `guard-L/R` (printed, `partNo: 'finger-guard'`); **signs** (6 mm ply, edged, four knob screws into tee-nuts in the web so nothing intrudes inside, to stay clear of the storage box walls); **grab handles** (bolted through the gunwale).
- **Frame:** hull-beam girders now start at the rear transom face; otherwise the rails and joists are unchanged and get joints.
