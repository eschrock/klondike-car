# Phase 3 round 2, F1 (front end): proposed DECISIONS text

| D-35 | **Front end, round 2: steel carries the pin; oak sees only bolt groups** (critic Phase 3 r1 MF-1…MF-4, build M11/M12).<br>- **Plates.** A ⅜" A36 carrier strip (2.5" × 11.94") lies on the beam top under the turntable; a 3/16" plate of the same footprint lies under the beam; a 3/16" plate (6" × 2") lies under the crossbar on the turntable top plate. All are waterjet/laser parts (`fab: outsource`, `sourcing`, `stock`). The carrier is the turntable mounting plate (M12): a 12" lazy susan's ~10" corner holes cannot land on the 4" beam, so the bearing is screwed to the carrier through two inner-flange holes (verify the bought bearing).<br>- **Pin.** ¾" grade 8 pin, unthreaded over the grip, in a 1¼" steel sleeve that runs plate to plate through the beam (spigoted into both). The pin is an overhanging beam: supports where the sleeve meets the carrier and beam plate (a = 2.78"), loaded by the crossbar plate b = 0.78" above the carrier. R_top = H(1+b/a) = 1.281H, R_bot = −0.281H, M = H·b. It touches no oak. The epoxy bed of the sleeve is a seal and is not counted. Crossbar sleeve dropped.<br>- **Oak sees only bolt groups.** Beam: six 5/16" grade 8 bolts in ONE line along the grain, 1.5625" (5D, l/D = 8) apart, x offset 0.125" so the front lip and the rod-groove edge are both 1.25" = 4D (⅜" bolts would need 1.5": do not fit). Crossbar: four bolts, 1.5" apart. Per-bolt capacity is NDS yield-mode Z (Im, Is, IIIs, IV; Fe⊥ = 6100·G^1.45/√D = 6,238 psi at 5/16", G = 0.68; steel Fe 87,000) × C_M 0.7 × C_D 1.6 (NDS 10.3.2 cap) × C_g 0.85 = 509 lbf at the carrier end. Row tear-out = n·F'v·t·s_crit (Appendix E) with F'v = 205 × 0.97 × 1.6. Group tear-out = row tear-out for a one-row group. Rider-independent distances (rows 5D, edges 4D, crossbar 4D/7D) are validator warnings, not check rows (a row exactly at its minimum reads marginal at every weight).<br>- **Cleavage.** NDS 3.4.3.3 V_r = ⅔F'v·b·d_e = 663 lbf for the beam front slab against about 1,280 lbf pushed by the carrier-end bolts: unreinforced it fails, so four ⅜" 316 stainless fore-aft cross-bolts (1¾" fender washers) are designed to carry ALL of it; two more across the crossbar. The 850 psi block-shear rows are gone (they were 3–4× unconservative).<br>- **Check rows.** `kingpin-plates` (steel: pin bending, sleeve and plate-hole bearing, spigots, plate tear-out, carrier strip), `kingpin-bolts` (NDS), `kingpin-split` (cleavage, cross-bolts, web), `steer-stops`, `steer-stops-bound`; `kingpin-peak` is an info row (replaced). `kingpin` / `kingpin-strike` / `roll-kingpin-uplift` read the plate model.<br>- **Limits.** Stainless (65 ksi) plate bolts fail the stop-torque row (use grade 8, zinc-flake, epoxy-sealed). A ¼" carrier fails it. The 6.25" turntable center hole is assumed (verify). Web of the stop channel touching the top plate under overturning (~460 lbf vertical) is not modeled; specify a UHMW pad. | 🔶 |
| D-36 | **Steering stops, round 2** (supersedes the oak dogs of D-31). Each dog is a 5/16" steel channel (outsourced, formed) standing on a short riser and a ½" arm that lie on the carrier INSIDE the turntable center hole (nothing through the ball ring: arm corner 3.08" < 3.125" hole radius, validated). Bonded ¼" 70–80A urethane pads are the stop faces; the oak stop blocks, planner and lock geometry are unchanged (W = 1.25/2 + 0.25 = 0.875"). **Share is computed, not assumed**: k = E·A/t = 1500 × (2.74 × 0.94)/0.25 = 15.4 kip/in per stop; share = ½ + k·δ/(2P) with δ = 0.005" → 0.52 at the goal rider (the wood faces of round 1 were ~480 kip/in, so a 0.005" mismatch closed one stop). Net kingpin force (2·share − 1)·P feeds `pinLoads`, so it enters every kingpin row. `steer-stops-bound` puts the whole torque on one stop: pad to its yield 1,500 psi, wood/steel/NDS to strength (allowable × SF). The stop torque reaches the beam as T·y/Σy² on the plate bolts (outermost 5.47") via the carrier in its own plane. Mass of the dogs (0.81 lb each) + arms. | 🔶 |
