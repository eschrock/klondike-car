## Peach State Lumber, Kennesaw — (770) 428-3622
- [ ] Is the online "Hardwoods LEVEL 2" sheet (FAS white oak 4/4 $8.25, 5/4 $10.00, 8/4 $12.50) the walk-in price? If not, what is?
- [ ] Is the white oak kiln-dried? FAS?
- [ ] Do they have 6/4 white oak (and at what price)? With 6/4 some laminations go away.
- [ ] Board plan (4/4 + 5/4 only), whole boards:
  - 4/4: six 6" x 10 ft, plus one 10" wide x 6 ft to resaw into the side band strips (about 35 bf)
  - 5/4: one 6" x 10 ft, plus two 7" wide x 6 ft to resaw into the rail strips (about 15 bf)
- [ ] Will they sell part of a board, or cut to length?
- [ ] While there: price of 1/4" plywood (birch, white oak) and of 1/2" (12 mm) marine okoume, 4 x 8.

## The Wood Yard
- [ ] 1/4" birch plywood, 4 x 8, $38: what is the core (all hardwood, poplar, lauan?), how many plies, and the glue (Type II)? Check an edge for voids. Bring calipers: measure the thickness (the dados are cut to it).
- [ ] 1/2" (12 mm) marine plywood, $90: confirm it is okoume, not meranti (meranti adds about 3 lb on the deck).
