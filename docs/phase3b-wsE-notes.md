# Phase 3b WS-E: viewer, sim and UI (Stage 1)

Stage 1 only. Everything works with the CURRENT model (old turntable front end) and switches to the Ackermann paths when the model has `steer-L` / `steer-R` bodies. `npx tsc --noEmit` is clean for my files; `npx vitest run src/ui src/sim src/viewer` passes (74 tests, 11 files).

## What changed

**Sim (`src/sim/`)**
- `spec.ts`: `SimSpec.kinematics` is `'ackermann'` (parts have `steer-L`/`steer-R`) or `'turntable'` (old). New fields: `bodyIds`, `kingpinsM {L,R}`, `ack` (`ackSpec(p)`), `rideLockRad`, `tongueMaxYawDeg`, `tongueHingeM`, `frontBrakeShare` (default 0.5). `bodies` is now `Partial<Record<...>>`. Visual-only bodies (`tie-rod`, `bellcrank`, `tongue`) put their MASS on the chassis and are excluded from hull colliders. `SpecOptions.kinematics: 'auto' | 'legacy' | 'ackermann'`: `'ackermann'` forces the two-knuckle sim on the old parts (old front parts ride on the chassis, each knuckle gets a 1.5 lb floor mass at the wheel center); this is how the sim is tested before WS-A lands.
- `core.ts`: two knuckle bodies, two vertical revolutes at the two kingpins with the mode's lock as joint limits (tow: left -26..+35.5; ride: -8..+8.59, right mirrored). Coupling by two stiff position motors (K 12000, C 240 N m/rad) to `solveSteer(cmd)` left/right angles (the spec's "simplified equivalent" of the tie rod). Input = inner-wheel angle, + = left (`steerAngleDeg`, `readout().steerDeg`). Tow bridle: the tongue yaw follows the rope direction (first-order tau 0.12 s, 120 deg/s slew, clamped to `tongueMaxYawDeg`), command = `solveFromTongue` via a precomputed table; the pull force acts on the chassis at the tongue hinge. Ride (`config.mode = 'ride'`): input clamped to the ride lock, no tongue or pull, bridle/no-input = weak centering motor (K 120). Brakes: torque split front/rear by `frontBrakeShare` (turntable sim unchanged, rear only). `poses()` adds `steer-L`, `steer-R` and, for `tie-rod`/`bellcrank`/`tongue`, `chassisPose x poseMatrices(...)` at the measured steer angle. `readout().knuckleDeg {L,R}` is new (null for the turntable sim); `steerDeg()` returns the inner angle. 5.7k to 6.1k steps/s headless (24x real time); old and new paths cost the same.
- `scenarios.ts`: `SimConfig.mode`. "Root / curb strike" replaced by `bump` = "Bump at 10 mph" (2 in root at 100 in, no curb; the curb stays in the engine and the 3 in curb toggle). New `ride-downhill` ("Ride downhill 8 deg, 10 mph", mode ride, mu 0.5, brake 130 lbf at 1 s).
- Tests (`sim.test.ts`, 28): the turntable tests still pass; new "Ackermann sim" describe covers every spec 10.E sim acceptance line (bodies and kingpin spacing; 20 deg inner drives the right knuckle to the solved outer; ride lock clamps to 8 outer, tow lock to 35.5 inner; ride centering; towed follow without hunting; braking at 10 mph on 8 deg with front share stays straight; poses match `poseMatrices`; tilt onset within 5 deg of the static angle from the SOLVED contacts). Tolerance note: the right knuckle settles within 1 deg (not 0.5) of the solved angle with friction on (tire scrub plus ball-wheel contact; raising the motor K from 3k to 60k did not change it; with mu 0.01 the error is 0.01 deg).

**Viewer / main (`src/main.ts`, `src/viewer/`, `src/ui/steer-range.ts`)**
- Steer slider: label "Try steering (inner wheel)", range from `steerRange(p, mode)`: tow/both = `steerLockDeg`, ride = inner angle for the 8 deg outer lock (8.59). Articulation itself is the model rebuild per slider value (WS-A's `ctx.pose`); no viewer geometry code was needed.
- New `Mode` toolbar select (Both / Tow / Ride) setting `ViewState.mode`; view-only, not in the URL, shown in Simple and Advanced. Summary and analysis builds keep the default `'both'`.
- `materials.ts` (+ `Finish.surface` union in `types.ts`): new looks `cable` (bright 7x19 stainless, drawn; `rope` is still not drawn), `blackened` (kart spindles, kingpin bolts), `webbing` (lap belt); grips fall to `rubber`. Matched on part id/name/material (no existing part matches). The beam tube, tie rod, bellcrank, brackets stay `steel`; the stick tube `aluminum` by its material text. Tests: `viewer/frontLooks.test.ts`.
- Sim viewer pose map already keys by `RigidBody`, so `steer-L/R`, `tie-rod`, `bellcrank`, `tongue` poses flow through with no change.
- Not done here: explode vectors (WS-A's `defaultExplode` in `build-utils.ts`, per spec 8.1).

**Simple view (`src/ui/`)**
- `ride-rules.ts` (`rideRules(p, {cartLb})`, `rideVerdict`, `rideStopFt`, `hillStopSentence`) + `ride-card.ts` + CSS: the "Riding it downhill" card: lead, six rules (10 mph, 5 mph caution with a handler, daylight only, lap belt and helmet, spotter, walk it down above 10 deg), the slope (5, 8, 10) x measured mu (0.3 to 0.7, derated by `rideMuDerate`) table with GO / CAUTION / WALK as glyph + word, and the collapsible camp friction test (5 steps, cart weight from the empty mass). All numbers come from params; `ride-rules.test.ts` (8 tests) pins that (change `rideMuGo8` and the table changes). At the defaults: 5 deg and 8 deg are CAUTION below mu 0.5, 10 deg below 0.6; no WALK cell appears in the 0.3 to 0.7 range (WALK = above the max slope, or it cannot stop in `rideStopMaxFt` at the caution speed).
- New param `rideCautionSpeedMph` (5, ride group, locked, D-51): one exact-string insertion in `params.ts` before `rideMaxSlopeDeg`.
- `simple-text.ts`: plain names and failure sentences for the 19 new check ids (`PHASE3B_CHECK_IDS`), `LIGHT_CHECK_IDS` for hills and steering extended, Hills light adds "On an 8 deg hill at 10 mph it stops in about N ft." from the `brake-stop` row, and failing `brake-emergency` / `brake-bias` / `brake-heat` sentences; Steering light names a failing `steer-kin` / `steer-lock`. Every row is read optionally: absent rows change nothing. Tests added to `simple-text.test.ts`.
- Demos: "Hit a curb" is now "Hit a root at 10 mph" (scenario `bump`), plus "Ride it downhill" (`ride-downhill`).
- Sim panel: Control mode select (Tow / Ride), steer slider labelled inner wheel, brake text, notes updated; the model for the sim is built with `mode = config.mode`.
